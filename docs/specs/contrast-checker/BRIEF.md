# WCAG Colour Contrast Checker — preparatory BRIEF v0.1

Specification documentation only; no product claim, readiness event or implementation. Full normative contract is SPEC.md.

## 1. Goal
Designers, front-end developers, teachers preparing slides and small businesses checking their own sites all meet the same question: is this text readable on this background — not by vibes, but by the WCAG number an auditor will use? Success: the user enters a foreground and a background colour (hex or rgb/rgba), immediately sees the exact contrast ratio computed by the WCAG 2.1 formula, pass/fail against AA and AAA for normal and large text plus the 3:1 non-text threshold, sample text rendered at both sizes — and, when a pair fails, a deterministic minimal correction suggestion (blend the foreground toward black or white in pinned 1% steps) instead of a shrug. The verdict is judged on the unrounded ratio, and the page says so, because a displayed 4.50 that is really 4.48 fails audits and misleads exactly the users who came for certainty.

## 2. Scope and functional distinction
Input formats closed: hex `#RGB`, `#RRGGBB`, `#RRGGBBAA` (case-insensitive, `#` optional in the text field), and `rgb()`/`rgba()` with integer channels 0–255 and alpha 0–1. Alpha is composited by a pinned order (background over white first, then foreground over the effective background, in sRGB space, luminance computed on the unrounded composite) so translucent colours get one reproducible answer. Formula pinned to WCAG 2.1: channel linearisation with the 0.03928 breakpoint, coefficients 0.2126/0.7152/0.0722, ratio (L1 + 0.05)/(L2 + 0.05). Thresholds: AA normal 4.5, AA large 3.0, AAA normal 7.0, AAA large 4.5, non-text 3.0 — large text defined on the page (≥24 px, or ≥18.66 px bold). Features: swap colours, sample previews, per-threshold pass/fail table, suggestion engine (direction selectable; first t in 0.01 steps whose unrounded ratio meets the chosen target; honest "no single-colour fix reaches AAA against this background" case). Excluded: palette generation or harmony design (a different tool, below), image sampling / eyedropper from uploaded pictures, colour-vision-deficiency simulation, batch/matrix checking of many pairs, and saving pairs.

Existing tools: Color Palette generates harmonious colour sets to use; this tool *evaluates* two given colours against a legalistic threshold set and repairs a failing foreground — generation vs judgement, disjoint operations on the same domain. No catalog entry, reserved specification or registry task at admission time computes WCAG ratios; fresh registry/path/draft check remains mandatory before admission. Fixtures are computed in the SPEC and re-derived during drafting (the boundary greys #767676 at 4.54 passing AA and #777777 at 4.48 failing are the canonical edge case, and the suggestion for #777777 lands exactly on #767676 at t = 0.01).
