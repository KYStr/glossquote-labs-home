# Placeholder Text Generator (Bilingual Lorem-Style Filler) — preparatory BRIEF v0.1

Specification documentation only; no product claim, readiness event or implementation. Full normative contract is SPEC.md.

## 1. Goal
Designers, developers and editors need filler that fills a layout honestly: text that looks like text at a glance, carries no meaning, and can never be mistaken for real content that someone forgot to replace. Success: the user picks a language (Latin lorem-style or Traditional Chinese filler), a unit (paragraphs, sentences, words, or exact characters), a count and a seed, and gets filler generated by one pinned algorithm from auditable pools — the classic lorem word pool for Latin, and a shipped pool of deliberately neutral, content-free zh sentences written for this tool — with the same settings always producing byte-identical output, a plain-text and HTML-paragraph copy option, and a permanent on-page label that this is placeholder text with no meaning. Reproducibility is the point a random filler site cannot offer: a mock shared with a client today regenerates identically next week from the seed printed beside it.

## 2. Scope and functional distinction
Algorithm pinned end-to-end: PRNG is mulberry32 over a uint32 seed (default 42, editable, "new seed" increments it), so output is a pure function of (language, unit, count, seed, classic-opening toggle). Latin model: sentences of 6–14 words drawn from the shipped lorem word pool, paragraphs of 3–6 sentences; the classic opening sentence (`Lorem ipsum dolor sit amet, consectetur adipiscing elit.`) may head the first paragraph (toggle, default on in paragraph mode). zh model: whole filler sentences drawn from the shipped zh sentence pool, paragraphs of 2–4 sentences; zh offers paragraphs/sentences/characters only — no "words" unit, because Chinese text has no word spaces and pretending otherwise would fake a count (stated on the page). Characters unit truncates to exactly N Unicode code points (which may cut a sentence mid-way — labelled as truncation). Output caps: 50 paragraphs / 200 sentences / 5,000 words / 20,000 characters. Copy as plain text or as `<p>`-wrapped HTML. Excluded: meaningful text generation of any kind (no AI writing, no marketing copy), other languages' filler (no "bacon ipsum" novelty pools), images, saving designs, and any styling beyond the copy formats.

Existing tools: Text Workbench transforms the user's own text; Word Frequency counts text; neither generates filler. No catalog entry, reserved specification or registry task at admission time generates placeholder text; fresh registry/path/draft check remains mandatory before admission. The honesty contract is the distinction that matters: pools ship as inspectable data, the seed is shown, and the non-meaning label is part of the output contract — fixtures are computed in the SPEC and re-derived during drafting (mulberry32 seed 42 draws are listed to 6 decimals, with the first sentence's length and first five word picks worked against a micro pool defined in the SPEC).
