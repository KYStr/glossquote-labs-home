# BMI and TDEE Calculator — preparatory BRIEF v0.1

Specification documentation only; no product claim, readiness event or implementation. Full normative contract is SPEC.md.

## 1. Goal
Almost everyone eventually types the same four numbers into a search box — height, weight, age, activity — and gets back a BMI and a daily calorie estimate from a page they cannot audit, often with the formula unnamed, the category boundary rounded the wrong way, or a diet plan attached. Success: the user enters sex, age, height and weight (metric or imperial), picks an activity level described in plain words, and receives BMI with its WHO category, the corresponding healthy weight range for their height, a Mifflin-St Jeor BMR, and a TDEE table for all five activity levels — every formula named on the page, every boundary rule stated, and a permanent, prominent statement that these are population estimates, not medical advice, and that the tool gives no eating targets. No account, no upload: the numbers never leave the page.

## 2. Scope and functional distinction
Pinned in the SPEC: BMI = kg/m² computed from unrounded values, displayed to one decimal (half away from zero), with the category judged on the **unrounded** value against the WHO bands (<18.5 underweight; [18.5, 25) normal; [25, 30) overweight; [30, 35) obese I; [35, 40) obese II; ≥40 obese III) — including the trap fixture 73.9 kg at 200 cm, which displays 18.5 yet is underweight, with the page stating the unrounded rule. An information-only line notes the Asia-Pacific public-health action points (23.0 / 27.5) without making them a second verdict. Healthy weight range for the entered height: [18.5·h², 25·h²) kg, lower bound displayed rounded, upper bound labelled "stay below". BMR by Mifflin-St Jeor (men +5, women −161), TDEE = unrounded BMR × one of five named multipliers (1.2 / 1.375 / 1.55 / 1.725 / 1.9), each activity level described behaviourally; all levels shown in one table, the selected one highlighted. Unit handling is exact: toggle metric (cm, kg) / imperial (ft+in, lb) with 1 in = 2.54 cm and 1 lb = 0.45359237 kg, all computation in unrounded metric. Accepted ranges: age 10–120 (under-18 shows a note that the equations are adult-derived), height 50–272 cm, weight 10–635 kg (post-conversion). Excluded: calorie targets for losing or gaining weight, meal plans, body-fat percentage, waist measures, pregnancy adjustments, saving or sharing results, and any diagnosis language — the category names are the WHO band labels, presented as bands, not findings.

Existing tools: no catalog entry, reserved specification or registry task computes BMI, BMR or TDEE; the nearest neighbours are generic calculators with different mathematics (mortgage amortisation — money over time; compound interest — growth over time), and the Grade Target / Subscription Budget tools share nothing but arithmetic. Fresh registry/path/draft check remains mandatory before admission. All fixtures — the two BMR/TDEE worked examples, the six boundary weights at 200 cm that land exactly on each band edge, the imperial 5 ft 9 in / 154 lb case, and the healthy-range figures — are re-derived by script during drafting.
