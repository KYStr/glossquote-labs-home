# Fuel Cost Calculator — preparatory BRIEF v0.1

Specification documentation only; no product claim, readiness event or implementation. Full normative contract is SPEC.md.

## 1. Goal
Drivers planning a trip face one arithmetic question — "how much fuel will this take, and what will it cost?" — buried under unit friction: the car's consumption may be quoted in L/100km, km/L, US MPG or UK MPG, the distance may be in kilometres or miles, and the pump price may be per litre or per gallon. Success: the user enters distance, consumption in any of the four units, and fuel price, and gets the exact fuel needed (litres), the total cost, and — as a secondary output — an optional per-person share when the cost is split among the car's occupants. All conversions use exact constants (1 mile = 1.609344 km; 1 US gallon = 3.785411784 L; 1 Imperial gallon = 4.54609 L) and all money arithmetic is exact rational arithmetic with half-up rounding only at display, so the same inputs give the same cents everywhere. No account, no saved trips, no location data: the page computes and forgets.

## 2. Scope and functional distinction
Pinned in the SPEC: distance in km or miles (0 < d ≤ 100,000 in the entered unit, up to two decimal places); consumption in L/100km (0.1–100), km/L (1–100), MPG US (1–200) or MPG UK (1–200), normalised to L/100km by the exact identities L/100km = 100 ÷ (km/L), L/100km = 235.2145833… ÷ MPG(US) and L/100km = 282.4809363… ÷ MPG(UK) (the constants are the exact fractions 112903/480 and 56826125/201168, never decimal approximations in the core); fuel price per litre, per US gallon or per Imperial gallon (0 < p ≤ 10,000 in the chosen currency, up to two decimal places) with a display-only currency selector (NT$, US$, €, £, ¥ — the symbol changes, no exchange conversion ever happens); optional split among 1–12 people, per-person share computed from the exact unrounded total and rounded half-up independently, with a visible note that rounded shares may sum a cent or two off the total. Outputs: fuel needed in litres (2 dp), total cost (2 dp), the normalised consumption echoed in L/100km (2 dp), and the per-person share when the split is on. Excluded: route planning or maps, vehicle databases, tolls and parking, EV charging, multi-leg trips, and saving trips.

Existing tools: Bill Splitter divides a known total among people with tip/tax options — it never computes the total from distance and consumption, which is this tool's primary job; the optional split here is a secondary display of an already-computed cost, with no tip/tax. Unit Converter converts single values between units but knows nothing of fuel, prices or trip cost. Electricity Cost Calculator prices appliance electricity from watts and hours, not vehicle fuel. No catalog entry, reserved specification or registry task computes trip fuel cost; fresh registry/path/draft check remains mandatory before admission. All fixtures — 250 km at 6.5 L/100km and $1.80/L (16.25 L, $29.25, $7.31 each for four), 100 miles at 40 MPG(US) and $3.60 per US gallon (exactly 2.5 gallons, $9.00), 300 km at 15 km/L and NT$30.50/L (20.00 L, NT$610.00), 200 miles at 50 MPG(UK) and £1.45/L (18.18436 L, £26.37), and a 3-way split of exactly $10.00 ($3.33 each, with the cent-difference note exercised) — are re-derived by script during drafting.
