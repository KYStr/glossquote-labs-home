# Meeting Cost Calculator — preparatory BRIEF v0.1

Specification documentation only; no product claim, readiness event or implementation. Full normative contract is SPEC.md.

## 1. Goal
Meetings feel free because nobody sees a meter running. This tool puts the meter on the wall: the organiser enters who is in the room — up to five groups of attendees, each a headcount and an hourly rate (typed directly, or filled from an annual-salary preset) — and sees the meeting's combined burn rate per hour, what a planned duration costs, and, in live mode, the cost accumulating second by second on a running timer. Success: before the meeting, a duration and a roster give an exact planned cost with a per-group breakdown; during the meeting, the live counter makes overrun tangible. The timer is honest about its model: it tracks elapsed time on a monotonic clock (pauses excluded, wall-clock changes powerless), and the cost is always the current burn rate times the total elapsed time — change the roster mid-meeting and the page says the whole elapsed time is re-priced at the new rate, not silently integrated. No account, no calendar access, no attendee names: the page computes and forgets.

## 2. Scope and functional distinction
Pinned in the SPEC: 1–5 attendee groups, each with a whole headcount 1–100 and an hourly rate 0–100,000 in the chosen display currency (at most two decimal places); a per-group preset selector fills the rate from an annual salary by the exact rule rate = annual ÷ 2080 (52 weeks × 40 hours), rounded half-up to cents at fill time, from the closed preset list 40,000 / 70,000 / 110,000 / 180,000 labelled as illustrative levels — after filling, the rate is an ordinary editable number. Burn = Σ headcount × rate. Manual mode: duration as hours (0–24) plus minutes (0–59), total 1–1,440 minutes, cost = burn × minutes ÷ 60 exactly. Timer mode: start/pause/reset on a monotonic time base; elapsed accumulates only while running; running cost = burn × elapsed_seconds ÷ 3600, recomputed on every display tick from total elapsed (never incremented by rounded amounts, so the displayed cents can never drift). Outputs: burn per hour, cost per minute, total cost (planned or live), and a per-group breakdown (headcount, rate, subtotal, per-person cost). Currency selector (NT$, US$, €, £, ¥) is a display symbol only. Excluded: calendar integration, named attendees or personal data, notifications, saved meetings, and any claim about what anyone "should" earn — presets are labelled illustrative.

Existing tools: Bill Splitter divides a known total; Work Hours Calculator totals one person's worked hours from clock times; neither prices a room of people over time or runs a live cost meter. No catalog entry, reserved specification or registry task computes meeting cost; fresh registry/path/draft check remains mandatory before admission. All fixtures — 3 people at $60/h plus 2 at $45/h (burn $270/h, 45 minutes → $202.50, $4.50 per minute), the 70,000 preset (rate $33.65, four people for 30 minutes → $67.30), timer readings at the same burn (600 s → $45.00, 90 s → $6.75), a one-person 8-hour day ($800.00), and a display-rounding case ($100/h for 100 s → $2.78) — are re-derived by script during drafting.
