# Compound Interest and Savings Goal Calculator — preparatory BRIEF v0.1

Specification documentation only; no product claim, readiness event or implementation. Full normative contract is SPEC.md.

## 1. Goal
Savers planning a deposit, a travel fund, an emergency cushion or a child's education pot ask two questions: if I put in this much every month at roughly this rate, where do I end up — and its mirror: to reach that target by then, how much per month is enough? Success: the user enters an initial amount, a nominal annual rate and a monthly contribution, and sees the balance month by month under one stated simulation (monthly compounding, contribution at month end, interest rounded to cents every month), a year-by-year summary of contributions vs interest vs balance, and the effective annual rate for honesty. In goal mode they enter a target instead, and get either the months required at their contribution, or the smallest monthly contribution (to the cent, found by a pinned binary search over the same simulation) that reaches the target in their chosen term. Every number on the page is reproducible from the simulation — never from a closed-form formula that disagrees with it by a cent and pretends otherwise.

## 2. Scope and functional distinction
Money is integer cents throughout (the discipline of the mortgage specification, applied to the opposite side of the ledger). Pinned model: monthly rate = nominal annual rate ÷ 12 (stated as nominal-with-monthly-compounding, not an APY conversion); each month interest = round-half-away-from-zero(balance × monthly rate) in cents, then the contribution is added (end-of-month timing, stated); term 1–600 months; amounts ≤ 999,999,999.99; rate 0–100% with up to 3 decimals. Outputs: final balance, total contributions, total interest, effective annual rate (1 + r/12)^12 − 1 displayed as information, a yearly table (year, contributed that year cumulative, interest cumulative, end balance), and a full monthly table on demand. Goal modes: (a) months-to-target at a fixed contribution (simulation result, with the honest outcome "not reached within 600 months" instead of an infinite promise); (b) required contribution for target + term by binary search over cents against the same simulation, verified minimal (one cent less falls short). Excluded: inflation adjustment, tax, irregular/one-off deposits, multiple accounts, loan or debt payoff (the mortgage tool's territory), currency conversion, and saving scenarios.

Existing tools: my own Mortgage Amortization Calculator specification is the debt mirror — amortising a liability to zero with annuity/equal-principal schedules; this tool grows an asset from contributions, with goal inversion (months-to-target, required contribution) that has no analogue there. Unit-price / bill-splitter tools do one-shot arithmetic, no time dimension. Initial catalog/spec/task scope contains no compound-growth or savings-goal calculator; fresh registry/path/draft check remains mandatory before admission. Fixtures are computed in the SPEC by the pinned simulation and re-derived during drafting — including the deliberate illustration that the closed-form formula gives 12,536.50 where the normative simulation gives 12,536.49, and the simulation governs.
