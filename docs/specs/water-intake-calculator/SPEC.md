# Daily Water Intake Calculator — full preparatory specification v0.1

Specification documentation only; no product claim, readiness event or implementation. This document is the normative contract a future production task must satisfy exactly; where it says MUST, a differing behaviour is a defect, not a variation. A health-adjacent calculator has two failure modes worse than a wrong number: presenting a population rule of thumb as a personal medical requirement, and quietly modelling things it must not (illness, pregnancy, medication). This specification therefore fixes exactly one small published-style model with its constants printed on the page, returns only a range, forbids condition inputs and diagnosis language anywhere in the product, and places a not-medical-advice statement next to every result.

## 1. Goal
Primary audience: adults who want a defensible daily water target range for ordinary life — office workers, recreational exercisers, people in hot climates — and who are better served by "2.1–2.5 litres for your inputs, here's the model" than by a universal glass count. The recurring problem: intake guidance is quoted as a single number detached from body size, activity and climate, and commercial pages often dress the estimate up as personalised health advice. Success is observable: the user enters weight, activity and climate and receives a range in millilitres and cups, computed under the exact model of section 4, with the model's constants visible on the same page and the not-medical-advice statement adjacent to the result. Non-goals: assessing the user's hydration state, adjusting for any medical condition, scheduling drinking, or tracking intake over time.

Assumptions: a modern desktop or mobile browser; adult users estimating for themselves (the page says the model is an adult rule of thumb); no network at runtime; nothing is stored — a person's weight is health-adjacent data and is treated accordingly.

## 2. Scope and functional distinction
Included: (a) three inputs — body weight (20–300 kg, 0.1 steps), activity level (four named levels with the flat additions of section 4), climate (three named bands with the multipliers of section 4); (b) the model of section 4 applied in its stated order; (c) outputs: the low–high range in millilitres (whole ml) and in 250 ml cups (one decimal), and the model statement itself rendered on the page from the same data block the core computes with; (d) the permanent not-medical-advice statement adjacent to the result (its required content is fixed in section 5). Excluded: age, sex, pregnancy, breastfeeding, illness, kidney or heart conditions, medication, altitude, or any other personal health factor as an input or adjustment; food and beverage water content accounting; per-hour or per-workout drinking schedules; reminders, logging or streaks; any wording that assesses the user ("you are dehydrated", "you drink too little") — the tool computes a range for stated inputs and judges nothing.
Distinction from existing work: BMI/TDEE Calculator estimates energy expenditure and a body-mass index from height/weight/age/sex — a different quantity with different inputs, and it likewise states it is not a diagnosis; this tool estimates a fluid range from weight alone plus two environmental factors and deliberately accepts *fewer* personal inputs. Pregnancy Due Date and Sleep Cycle calculators are date/time tools with no physiological model. No catalog entry, reserved specification or registry task at admission time computes water intake; the admission check MUST be re-run against the fresh registry before production, and a collision stops the task for coordinator decision rather than a rename.

## 3. User flow and lifecycle
Initial state: the three inputs prefilled with the worked example (70 kg, sedentary, temperate) and the result already showing fixture W1's range, so the model and the disclaimer are visible before the user changes anything. Editing any input recomputes the range together. An out-of-range weight (below 20, above 300, or more than one decimal) is a field error: the range freezes at its last valid state marked out of date, with the bound stated. Activity and climate are selects and cannot be invalid. The not-medical-advice statement sits directly under the result at all times (including the frozen state) and is part of both language versions; it is never dismissible, never behind a toggle, and never replaced by a shorter version. There is no submit button and no history: the page is a live calculator. Nothing persists across reload; the weight never appears in the URL or title.

## 4. Computation rules (normative)
Model constants (normative data; MUST ship once as data, match this section exactly, and be rendered on the page):
- Baseline: low rate 30 ml/kg, high rate 35 ml/kg.
- Activity additions (ml, added to both ends): sedentary 0; light 350; moderate 700; high 1,050.
- Climate multipliers: temperate 1.0; warm 1.1; hot 1.25.
Formula (in this order, exact decimal arithmetic): lowExact = (30 × weightKg + activityMl) × climate; highExact = (35 × weightKg + activityMl) × climate.
Display: millilitres rounded half-up to whole ml, applied once per end. Cups: cupsLow = lowExact ÷ 250 and cupsHigh = highExact ÷ 250 computed from the *exact* values (not from the rounded ml), displayed at one decimal, half-up. The range is always shown low–high; the ends are never collapsed into a single "target" figure anywhere in the product (including headings, meta descriptions and the page title — a single-number summary is a defect).
Bounds: weight 20.0–300.0 kg (0.1 steps). At the extremes the model still applies unchanged (300 kg, high, hot → 12,563–14,063 ml): the figure is shown with the model and disclaimer, and the page does not editorialize about it.
Fixtures (independently derived; implementations MUST reproduce exactly):
- W1: 70 kg, sedentary, temperate → 2,100–2,450 ml; cups 8.4–9.8.
- W2: 70 kg, moderate, temperate → 2,800–3,150 ml; cups 11.2–12.6.
- W3: 60 kg, light, warm → (1,800+350)×1.1 = 2,365 and (2,100+350)×1.1 = 2,695 → 2,365–2,695 ml; cups 9.5–10.8.
- W4 (rounding): 80 kg, high, hot → (2,400+1,050)×1.25 = 4,312.5 → 4,313 and (2,800+1,050)×1.25 = 4,812.5 → 4,813 ml; cups from exact values 17.3–19.3.

## 5. Implementation and safety
Architecture (future production): a pure core (the section 4 formula and formatting) with zero DOM access; a controller; a thin DOM layer. The constants ship once as an auditable data block matching section 4, and the on-page model statement is generated from that same block so the printed model cannot diverge from the computed one. Runtime dependencies: none.
Privacy/security: no network, no storage, no analytics; body weight is never transmitted, logged or placed in the URL/title; there is no free text to inject; no eval/Function; no external resources.
Honesty rules (normative, testable): (1) The not-medical-advice statement MUST appear adjacent to the result in both languages with this content: this is a general educational estimate from a simple model; individual needs vary with health, medication, diet and many factors the tool does not model; it is not medical advice; anyone with a health condition or concern should ask a qualified professional. (2) No diagnosis or assessment language anywhere in the product strings: an automated string audit in the test suite searches both locales for a forbidden list (including "dehydrated", "you should drink exactly", "your requirement is", and their zh-TW equivalents) and fails on any hit outside the disclaimer itself. (3) The result is always a range; the constants are printed; no authority ("doctors recommend", a named institution) is invoked — the model is presented as a rule of thumb, nothing more.
Bilingual and accessibility: full zh-TW (default) and English UI, including the model statement and the disclaimer. Native labelled inputs and selects; the range is text with its unit; the disclaimer is ordinary readable text (not fine print below body size); keyboard-complete flow. Performance bounds: three-input arithmetic per edit. Toolchain: Node 24, zero npm dependencies, house layout (public/, public/en/, core modules, node:test suites, build/check/serve scripts); runs offline from file:// or any static host.

## 6. Independent nine-aspect product rubric
Each aspect is scored 0–100 by a reviewer who is not the author, against the same fixed candidate commit; every aspect MUST reach ≥90 with all mandatory checks complete, otherwise the verdict is revision. Unrun checks stay NOT_RUN and block acceptance.
- functionality: both languages; fixtures W1–W4 reproduced exactly, including cups computed from exact values and the W4 half-up cases; the on-page model statement matches the shipped constants.
- boundaries: weight 20.0 and 300.0 accepted, 19.9, 300.1 and two-decimal weights refused with the freeze behaviour; every activity and climate selection exercised.
- privacy: zero network after load; no storage; weight absent from URL/title and never transmitted.
- security: no external resources; no eval/Function; no injectable free text exists.
- usability: a first-time user obtains their range and can point to where the model and the disclaimer are stated (spot-check); the range is never mistaken for a single prescribed number in the layout.
- accessibility: labelled native controls; result and disclaimer are readable text at body size or larger; keyboard-complete.
- localization-seo: zh-TW and en pages complete and equivalent (including model statement and disclaimer); meta descriptions present the tool as a range estimator, never a single figure; correct lang attributes, titles and same-language home links.
- toolchain-provenance: zero-dependency build reproduces byte-identical output on Node 24; the constants appear once as auditable data matching section 4 and the rendered model statement is generated from that same data (a build check compares them); Node 24.
- compatibility: current Chrome/Edge/Firefox/Safari desktop and mobile-width layout; fixtures give identical results across browsers.

## 7. Required tests and timing
Core unit tests with independently derived expectations: (a) fixtures W1–W4 exactly, ml and cups; (b) order-of-operations: a case distinguishing (base + activity) × climate from base × climate + activity (W3 serves), asserted explicitly; (c) cups computed from exact values, not rounded ml (a case where the two differ at the displayed decimal is constructed and asserted); (d) the string audit of section 5 over both locales' string tables, including meta text; (e) bounds: weight limits at and beyond, freeze behaviour; (f) the disclaimer presence check: the rendered page (both languages) contains the full disclaimer adjacent to the result block (DOM structure test).
GUI/evidence tests (desktop, real browser): entering W2's inputs yields 2,800–3,150; the disclaimer is visible without scrolling past the result on a mobile-width viewport; no single-number summary appears in the title or headings. Timing: full core suite under 60 seconds; no network in any test.
Explicitly NOT_RUN at specification stage: all product tests above; this document claims document completeness only.
