# Paint Coverage Calculator — preparatory BRIEF v0.1

Specification documentation only; no product claim, readiness event or implementation. Full normative contract is SPEC.md.

## 1. Goal
Anyone about to buy paint for a room faces the same chain of arithmetic: four walls from a rectangular room, minus the doors and windows, times the number of coats, divided by the coverage printed on the can — then the awkward second question, "so how many cans, and how much will be left over?" Success: the user enters room length, width and wall height, counts of standard doors (0.9 × 2.1 m) and windows (1.5 × 1.2 m) plus an optional custom opening area, the number of coats, and the paint's coverage in m²/L, and gets the paintable area, the exact litres needed, and the whole-can purchase for a chosen can size with the leftover stated. An optional ceiling toggle adds the ceiling area at the same coat count. All arithmetic is exact rational arithmetic; the only rounding is half-up at display and the ceiling function on cans — a can count is never rounded down. No account, no saved rooms, no product catalogue: the page computes and forgets.

## 2. Scope and functional distinction
Pinned in the SPEC: a single rectangular room, length and width 0.1–100 m, wall height 0.1–10 m; gross wall area exactly 2 × (L + W) × H; deductions of exactly 1.89 m² per door and 1.8 m² per window (counts 0–20 each) plus a custom openings area 0–1,000 m²; deductions reaching or exceeding the gross wall area are an explicit error state, never a silent zero. Ceiling toggle adds exactly L × W. Coats are whole, 1–4. Coverage is 1–30 m²/L. Required litres = paintable area × coats ÷ coverage, exact. Purchase: one can size chosen from the closed list 0.5 / 1 / 2.5 / 5 / 10 L; cans = the exact mathematical ceiling of litres ÷ size (an exactly divisible litres value buys exactly that many cans, never one more); leftover = cans × size − exact litres. Excluded: non-rectangular rooms and sloped ceilings, walls measured individually, primer as a separate product, texture/roughness adjustments, sprayer loss factors, prices and brand databases.

Existing tools: Tile Estimator counts floor tiles, spacing and grout for a tiled floor — discrete units on a floor, nothing to do with liquid coverage on walls. Box Fit checks whether items fit inside a box volume. Cut Planner lays out cuts on sheet material. No catalog entry, reserved specification or registry task computes paint quantity; fresh registry/path/draft check remains mandatory before admission. All fixtures — a 4 × 5 m room at 2.8 m height with 1 door and 2 windows (gross 50.40 m², net 44.91 m², 8.982 L for 2 coats at 10 m²/L, two 5 L cans, 1.02 L leftover), the same room with ceiling (64.91 m², 12.98 L, three 5 L cans), an exactly divisible case (3 × 3 m at 2.5 m, one coat → exactly 3.00 L → exactly three 1 L cans, leftover 0.00), a small one-coat room (2.05 L → one 2.5 L can, 0.45 L leftover), and the openings-exceed-walls error case — are re-derived by script during drafting.
