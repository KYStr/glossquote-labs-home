# Typing Speed Test — preparatory BRIEF v0.1

Specification documentation only; no product claim, readiness event or implementation. Full normative contract is SPEC.md.

## 1. Goal
People preparing for data-entry jobs, or just curious about their keyboard skill, take typing tests on ad-choked sites that each compute "WPM" differently and quietly upload keystroke data. Success: the user picks a duration (30 or 60 seconds) and a passage language (English or Traditional Chinese), types into a focused field while the passage marks each character correct or incorrect live, and at the end sees an honest, reproducible result — gross and net WPM for English, characters per minute for Chinese, accuracy, and a per-word/per-character error review — computed by the exact formulas in the SPEC, locally, with nothing about their typing leaving the page.

## 2. Scope and functional distinction
Fixed, bundled passages only: a set of English passages (public-domain or originally written for the tool, each 600–900 characters, plain prose without digits-heavy or symbol-heavy traps unless labelled) and Traditional Chinese passages (original or public-domain, 300–500 characters, no rare characters, punctuation limited to the stated set). The test starts on the first keystroke, ends exactly at the chosen duration (input locks; the character under the cursor at expiry is not counted), and cannot be paused — leaving the page or switching mode abandons the attempt with a stated confirmation. Scoring is fully specified: for Latin text, gross WPM = (all typed characters ÷ 5) ÷ minutes and net WPM = gross WPM × accuracy, accuracy = correct characters ÷ all typed characters, with spaces counted as characters and correctness judged character by character against the passage (an incorrect character stays incorrect even if later characters realign — the SPEC fixes the alignment rule: comparison is strictly positional, backspace is allowed before moving on but the SPEC must state exactly how corrections are scored); for Chinese text, the headline number is correct characters per minute with the same positional rule over characters, and no fake "WPM" conversion is displayed. Results include a review mode colouring the passage by correctness and listing the mistyped words/characters with counts. Excluded: free-text import, custom passages, multiplayer/leaderboards/accounts, keystroke logging beyond the current attempt, sound effects, and mobile/touch keyboards as a scored environment (the page works, but results are labelled desktop-keyboard oriented).

Existing tools: Focus Timer measures minutes; Text Workbench transforms text; nothing in the catalog times typed input against a reference passage or scores positional accuracy. Initial catalog/spec/task scope contains no typing test; fresh registry/path/draft check remains mandatory before admission. Passage provenance matters: every bundled passage ships with its source/author recorded in a PASSAGES.md, public-domain or original only — no scraped lyrics or book excerpts under copyright.
