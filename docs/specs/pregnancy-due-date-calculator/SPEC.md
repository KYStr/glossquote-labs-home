# Pregnancy Due Date Calculator — full preparatory specification v0.1

Specification documentation only; no product claim, readiness event or implementation. This document is the normative contract a future production task must satisfy exactly; where it says MUST, a differing behaviour is a defect, not a variation. Due-date arithmetic fails in two distinct ways this specification guards against: calendrical slips (month lengths, leap years, off-by-one trimester boundaries) and overconfidence — a single date presented as a prediction when it is a statistical convention. The specification therefore pins one day-zero reduction for all three dating modes, exact civil-date arithmetic, and a permanent not-medical-advice label that is itself a testable requirement.

## 1. Goal
Primary audience: a person who has just learned they are pregnant and the people around them, checking dates at home before or between clinical appointments. The recurring problem: Naegele's rule (add 280 days to the last menstrual period) is simple to state and surprisingly easy to misapply by hand — the old "add 7 days, subtract 3 months" shortcut drifts around month lengths and leap years, cycle lengths other than 28 days shift the result, and none of the hand methods tells you the trimester dates or today's gestational age without more counting. Success is observable: in any of the three dating modes the user receives the estimated due date (EDD), the gestational age as of today in weeks and days, the current trimester, and the calendar start/end dates of all three trimesters — always next to a plain statement that this is a rough estimate and not medical advice. Non-goals: dating a pregnancy clinically, predicting the actual day of birth, or any health guidance; the tool does standard calendar arithmetic and labels it as such.

Assumptions: a modern desktop or mobile browser; the user knows at least one of the three dating inputs (LMP, conception date, or an ultrasound date with its measured gestational age); all dates are the user's local civil dates — no times, no time zones; no network at runtime; no date is stored or transmitted.

## 2. Scope and functional distinction
Included: (a) three dating modes selected by a toggle — last menstrual period (LMP), conception date, ultrasound; (b) LMP mode fields: LMP date, cycle length (whole days, 21–45, default 28); (c) conception mode field: conception date; (d) ultrasound mode fields: scan date, gestational age at scan as whole weeks (4–42) plus days (0–6); (e) outputs, identical in shape for every mode: estimated due date; gestational age today (weeks+days, with the "today" date shown); current trimester (first/second/third, or the beyond-42-weeks note of section 4); a three-row trimester table giving each trimester's start and end calendar dates; the implied day zero is not displayed (it is an internal anchor, not a meaningful date for the user — in conception mode it is not the conception date, and showing it would invite confusion). Excluded: entering a known due date to work backwards; IVF or embryo-transfer dating; adjustments for twins or other clinical factors; fetal growth, weight or development content; reminders, appointments or any saving of dates; links to medical services.
Distinction from existing work: Date Calculator performs generic calendar arithmetic between two dates; it embeds no obstetric convention — not Naegele's rule, not the 266-day conception offset, not cycle adjustment, not gestational-age or trimester semantics — and it carries no medical-estimate labelling duty, which is a normative, tested part of this tool. The Sleep Cycle Calculator plans clock times within a night; the Running Pace Calculator predicts race times from a performance model — both irrelevant here. No catalog entry, reserved specification or registry task at admission time dates a pregnancy; the admission check MUST be re-run against the fresh registry before production, and a collision stops the task for coordinator decision rather than a rename.

## 3. User flow and lifecycle
Initial state: LMP mode selected with the cycle length at its default 28 and the LMP date empty (no result until a date is entered — the page never pre-fills a plausible-looking pregnancy). Entering a valid date computes all outputs together. Switching modes preserves nothing between modes except the mode itself during the page session: each mode's fields are independent, and switching back restores that mode's last entries for the session only.
The not-medical-advice notice is present from first load, sits next to the results whenever results are shown, and cannot be dismissed — its presence and wording keys are part of the acceptance tests, not a footer nicety.
Error states: a date in the future (after the device's today) is refused with a plain message; a malformed or impossible date (e.g. 2026-02-30) is refused by the date control's own parsing plus the core check; cycle length non-whole or outside 21–45 refused; ultrasound weeks outside 4–42 or days outside 0–6 refused. While an error stands, results freeze at the last valid state marked "out of date", or stay empty if there has been no valid state. Nothing persists across reload; dates never appear in the URL or title — a deliberate privacy property for health-adjacent data.

## 4. Computation rules (normative)
All arithmetic is on civil dates in the proleptic Gregorian calendar, implemented as a days-from-civil conversion (or exactly equivalent date-only arithmetic): no timestamps, no time zones, no daylight-saving edge cases can exist. Day counting is exclusive-of-start: date + N days means N midnights later, matching ordinary calendar addition (2026-01-01 + 1 = 2026-01-02).
Mode reductions to an estimated due date (EDD):
- LMP mode: EDD = LMP + 280 + (cycle − 28) days. At the default cycle 28 this is exactly Naegele's rule.
- Conception mode: EDD = conception + 266 days.
- Ultrasound mode: EDD = scan_date + (280 − GA_scan_days) days, where GA_scan_days = 7 × weeks + days at the scan.
Derived values (identical for every mode): day_zero = EDD − 280 days. Gestational age today: GA_days = today − day_zero (today = the device's local civil date at load, displayed alongside the result); expressed as ⌊GA_days ÷ 7⌋ weeks and GA_days mod 7 days. Trimester of a GA day count: first trimester = days 0–97 (0w0d–13w6d); second = days 98–195 (14w0d–27w6d); third = day 196 (28w0d) onward. Trimester calendar boundaries: first = [day_zero, day_zero + 97]; second = [day_zero + 98, day_zero + 195]; third = [day_zero + 196, EDD]. Current trimester is the one containing GA_days; when GA_days > 294 (42w0d), the current-trimester line is replaced by the note that the dates imply more than 42 weeks and should be checked — the GA figure itself is still shown, never clamped or hidden. GA_days cannot be negative under the section 3 validation (every entered date is ≤ today, and each mode's day_zero is ≤ its entered date — for the ultrasound mode day_zero = scan − GA_scan_days ≤ scan ≤ today).
Worked fixtures (normative, re-derived by script during drafting; gestational-age fixtures use the fixed reference date 2026-11-02 as "today" — production uses the device date, tests inject the reference date):
- D1: LMP 2026-01-01, cycle 28 → EDD **2026-10-08**; trimester dates: first 2026-01-01 → **2026-04-08**, second **2026-04-09** → **2026-07-15**, third from **2026-07-16** → 2026-10-08.
- D2: LMP 2026-01-01, cycle 35 → EDD **2026-10-15** (the +7 adjustment, nothing else changes shape).
- D3: conception 2026-01-15 → EDD **2026-10-08** — identical to D1, as it must be: a 28-day-cycle LMP implies conception at LMP + 14, and 280 − 14 = 266. This cross-mode consistency is a normative property, not a coincidence.
- D4: ultrasound scan 2026-03-05, GA at scan 9w2d (65 days) → EDD = scan + 215 = **2026-10-06**.
- D5: LMP 2026-10-20, cycle 28, today = 2026-11-02 → GA_days = 13 → **1 week 6 days**, first trimester; EDD **2027-07-27**.
- D6: LMP 2027-11-15, cycle 28 → EDD **2028-08-21** (the interval crosses 2028-02-29; a 365-day-year implementation lands a day early and fails this fixture).

## 5. Implementation and safety
Architecture (future production): a pure core (days-from-civil and civil-from-days conversions, the three mode reductions, GA and trimester derivation) with zero DOM access and zero use of timestamp-based Date arithmetic for the computation itself (the device date may be read once, as a civil date, to obtain "today"); a controller; a thin DOM layer. The constants 280, 266, 98, 196 and the input bounds appear once as auditable data matching section 4. Runtime dependencies: none.
Privacy/security: this tool handles health-adjacent dates; the bar is explicit — no network, no storage of any kind (not even localStorage), no analytics; dates never enter the URL, the title or any log; inputs render as text only; no eval/Function; no external resources.
Honesty rules (normative, testable): the not-medical-advice notice — stating that the due date is a statistical estimate, that only a minority of births happen on the due date itself, that cycle-based dating is less reliable than an early ultrasound, and that a clinician's dating prevails — is permanently visible, appears in both languages with equivalent meaning, and is never dismissible, collapsed or shown only once; the words "predicted birth date" or equivalents that overstate certainty are forbidden in UI copy: the output is always labelled "estimated due date".
Bilingual and accessibility: full zh-TW (default) and English UI, including the notice, the beyond-42-weeks note and the trimester table. Dates use native date inputs with text labels; results and the trimester table are real text/table markup with headers; keyboard-complete flow; recomputed results announced politely as text.
Performance bounds: a few integer operations per edit. Toolchain: Node 24, zero npm dependencies, house layout (public/, public/en/, core modules, node:test suites, build/check/serve scripts); runs offline from file:// or any static host.

## 6. Independent nine-aspect product rubric
Each aspect is scored 0–100 by a reviewer who is not the author, against the same fixed candidate commit; every aspect MUST reach ≥90 with all mandatory checks complete, otherwise the verdict is revision. Unrun checks stay NOT_RUN and block acceptance.
- functionality: both languages and all three modes; fixtures D1–D6 reproduced exactly, including trimester boundary dates and the D1/D3 cross-mode identity.
- boundaries: cycle 21 and 45 accepted, 20, 46 and 28.5 refused; ultrasound GA 4w0d and 42w6d accepted, 3w6d, 43w0d and 7 days refused; a date one day in the future refused; 2026-02-30 refused; GA beyond 42 weeks shown with its note, unclamped.
- privacy: zero network after load; zero storage (localStorage/sessionStorage/cookies all verified untouched); entered dates absent from URL/title; nothing transmitted.
- security: field content containing markup renders inert; no external resources; no eval/Function.
- usability: a first-time user with an LMP date gets a due date and today's gestational age without instructions, and can say which trimester they are in and when it ends (spot-check).
- accessibility: native labelled date inputs; the trimester table is a real table with headers; the notice readable as text; keyboard-complete; results announced politely.
- localization-seo: zh-TW and en pages complete and equivalent, with the not-medical-advice notice carrying equal prominence and meaning in both; correct lang attributes, titles, descriptions and same-language home links.
- toolchain-provenance: zero-dependency build reproduces byte-identical output on Node 24; the constants 280/266/98/196 and bounds appear once as auditable data matching section 4; computation demonstrably timestamp-free (fixtures pass with the process timezone varied).
- compatibility: current Chrome/Edge/Firefox/Safari desktop and mobile-width layout; identical outputs for the fixture corpus across browsers and across device time zones (civil dates only).

## 7. Required tests and timing
Core unit tests with independently derived expectations: (a) fixtures D1–D6 exactly — EDD, all six trimester boundary dates for D1, GA weeks+days for D5 under the injected reference date; (b) calendar integrity: days-from-civil round-trips across leap and non-leap Februaries (2028-02-29 exists, 2026-02-29 does not), century rules (2000 leap, 2100 not) — and the whole fixture corpus re-run under at least two different process time zones with identical results; (c) consistency properties: for random valid LMP/cycle pairs, conception mode with conception = LMP + (cycle − 14) reproduces the same EDD; at core level (bypassing the UI's 4-week input floor, which the core does not impose on the reduction itself), ultrasound reduction with scan = LMP and GA = 0 days reproduces the LMP EDD; (d) validation: future dates in every mode, impossible dates, cycle and GA bounds at and beyond their limits; (e) the beyond-42-weeks note triggers at GA_days = 295 and not at 294.
GUI/evidence tests (desktop, real browser): fixtures entered through the real UI in both languages and all modes; the notice located next to the results and verified non-dismissible; mode switching preserving each mode's own fields for the session; stale-state behaviour on a future date. Timing: full core suite under 60 seconds; no network in any test; storage APIs asserted unused.
Explicitly NOT_RUN at specification stage: all product tests above; this document claims document completeness only.
