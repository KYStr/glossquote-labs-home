# Pregnancy Due Date Calculator — preparatory BRIEF v0.1

Specification documentation only; no product claim, readiness event or implementation. Full normative contract is SPEC.md.

## 1. Goal
A newly pregnant person (or their partner) wants three numbers immediately: the estimated due date, how far along the pregnancy is today, and when each trimester starts and ends. The arithmetic behind them is standard but easy to get wrong by hand: Naegele's rule counts 280 days from the first day of the last menstrual period (LMP), assumes a 28-day cycle, and has to cross month lengths and leap years without slipping. Success: the user picks one of three dating modes — LMP (with an optional cycle-length adjustment), a known conception date, or an ultrasound scan date with the gestational age measured at that scan — and gets the estimated due date (EDD), the gestational age as of today in weeks+days, the current trimester, and the calendar dates of all three trimester boundaries. A permanent, non-dismissible notice states that this is a rough estimate, that most births do not occur on the due date, and that only a clinician can date a pregnancy — the labelling is part of the specification, not decoration. No account, no saved dates, no health data leaves the page: everything is date-only arithmetic on the device.

## 2. Scope and functional distinction
Pinned in the SPEC: all arithmetic is civil-date (proleptic Gregorian) day counting with no time zones or times of day. LMP mode: EDD = LMP + 280 + (cycle − 28) days, cycle whole 21–45, default 28 (unadjusted Naegele). Conception mode: EDD = conception + 266 days. Ultrasound mode: EDD = scan date + (280 − GA at scan) days, GA at scan whole weeks 4–42 plus days 0–6. Every mode reduces to an implied day zero (EDD − 280), from which everything else derives: gestational age today = today − day zero, expressed as weeks+days; trimester boundaries fixed at day 98 (14w0d, second trimester begins) and day 196 (28w0d, third begins), with the first trimester spanning days 0–97 and the second 98–195; all boundary calendar dates are shown. Entered dates must not be in the future; a gestational age beyond 42 weeks is displayed with a "check the dates" note rather than hidden or clamped. Excluded: reverse calculation from a known due date, multiple pregnancy adjustments, IVF/embryo-transfer dating, growth or weight estimates, appointment scheduling, and any medical content beyond the honesty notice.

Existing tools: Date Calculator adds and subtracts days between arbitrary dates but embeds no obstetric rule — it does not know Naegele's rule, the 266-day conception offset, cycle adjustment, gestational-age semantics or trimester boundaries, and carries no medical-estimate labelling obligation. No catalog entry, reserved specification or registry task dates a pregnancy; fresh registry/path/draft check remains mandatory before admission. All fixtures — LMP 2026-01-01 at cycle 28 → EDD 2026-10-08 (trimester boundaries 2026-04-08 / 2026-04-09 / 2026-07-15 / 2026-07-16), the same LMP at cycle 35 → 2026-10-15, conception 2026-01-15 → the same EDD 2026-10-08 (cross-mode consistency), an ultrasound at 2026-03-05 measuring 9w2d → EDD 2026-10-06, gestational age 1w6d on the fixed reference date 2026-11-02 for LMP 2026-10-20, and a leap-crossing case (LMP 2027-11-15 → EDD 2028-08-21) — are re-derived by script during drafting.
