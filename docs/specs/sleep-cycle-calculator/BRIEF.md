# Sleep Cycle Calculator — preparatory BRIEF v0.1

Specification documentation only; no product claim, readiness event or implementation. Full normative contract is SPEC.md.

## 1. Goal
People who have absorbed the popular "sleep in 90-minute cycles" idea still have to do error-prone clock arithmetic backwards from an alarm time, across midnight, in their head, at midnight. Success: the user enters a wake-up time and gets the bedtimes that land the alarm at the end of a whole number of cycles — or enters a bedtime (or taps "I'm going to sleep now") and gets the matching wake times — with the time needed to fall asleep counted honestly instead of absorbed into the first cycle, every suggested time labelled with its cycle count, total sleep, and whether it falls on the previous evening, the same morning, or the next day. No account, no tracking, no saved sleep data: the page computes clock arithmetic and forgets it.

## 2. Scope and functional distinction
Pinned in the SPEC: one cycle is exactly 90 minutes by definition of the tool (the page says cycle lengths vary between people and across the night — 90 minutes is the conventional planning average, not a measurement of the user); fall-asleep latency is a whole number of minutes, 0–60, default 15, and is always added outside the cycles (bedtime suggestions subtract it, wake suggestions add it, and both directions show "time in bed" = latency + cycles × 90 separately from "sleep" = cycles × 90, so the two numbers can never be confused). Suggestions are exactly four rows, for 6, 5, 4 and 3 cycles in the bedtime direction (9 h, 7 h 30, 6 h, 4 h 30 of sleep) and 3, 4, 5, 6 in the wake direction, with the 5- and 6-cycle rows visually marked as the commonly recommended range and a plain note that individual needs vary and this is not medical advice. All arithmetic is minutes modulo 1440 from a 24-hour HH:MM input; a result that wraps past midnight carries a day label (previous evening / same day / next day) derived from the unwrapped value, never guessed from the clock face. A "sleep now" action in the wake direction uses the device's current local time as the bedtime. Excluded: dates and calendars, alarms (the tool sets nothing), sleep tracking or history, REM/deep-sleep claims, naps and polyphasic schedules, smartwatch data, and any medical recommendation beyond the general note.

Existing tools: Date Calculator adds and subtracts calendar days and knows nothing of within-day cycle planning; Focus Timer counts down work sessions in real time (a live timer, not a what-time-should-I question); the Metronome schedules audio clicks. No catalog entry, reserved specification or registry task plans times by sleep cycles; fresh registry/path/draft check remains mandatory before admission. All fixtures — the four bedtimes for a 07:00 wake-up at the default latency (21:45 and 23:15 previous evening, 00:45 and 02:15 same morning), the four wake times from a 23:00 bedtime (03:45–08:15 next day), and a zero-latency case — are re-derived by script during drafting.
