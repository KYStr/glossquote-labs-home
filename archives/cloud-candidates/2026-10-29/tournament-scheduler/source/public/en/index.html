How it works

How are byes handled?

With an odd team count, one team has a bye in each round. Each team gets one bye in a single round robin and two in a double round robin. Even team counts have no byes. Identical inputs produce identical fixtures.

Are teams saved or uploaded?

No. Data stays in this page’s memory, with no account, storage, telemetry or external API. Loading the static page initially still needs network access. Leaving, refreshing or clearing loses the draft. Copies and downloads are managed by you and your browser.

Is this a fair venue or home/away allocation?

No. Every pair meets once in single mode and twice in double mode, but venues, time slots, rest intervals and home/away fairness are not optimized. This tool does not record scores or standings.

Clear the teams and schedule?

This removes all current inputs and results and cannot be undone. Downloads or clipboard writes already handed to your browser cannot be recalled.
