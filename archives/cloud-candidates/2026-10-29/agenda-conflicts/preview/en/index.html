Enter sessions for one day in one shared time basis. Check double-booked rooms or speakers without automatically moving any session.

Rules and common questions

Do touching sessions overlap?

No. Intervals include the start and exclude the end: 09:00–10:00 and 10:00–11:00 do not overlap. A positive same-room buffer still flags this pair as insufficient turnover time.

How do the two buffers apply?

The room buffer applies only to the same room. The speaker buffer applies only to a shared speaker moving between different rooms, not within one room. Actual overlaps are flagged as overlaps, not again as buffer failures. For non-overlapping sessions, a gap below the buffer fails; an equal gap passes.

Are duplicate sessions removed?

No. Equal titles, rooms, times and speaker sets are flagged as duplicate rows, with any other conflict reasons retained. Each pair is counted once even when it has several reasons.

How much can I check?

Use 1–60 rows with 0–5 speakers per row, separated by semicolons; a row cannot repeat a speaker. All 1770 possible pairs are checked and reported by original row numbers without silent truncation. Titles allow 80 UTF-16 units, room/speaker names 40, and every raw field at most 256.

Does a clean result validate my whole event plan?

No. This tool compares only the same-day minutes and names you enter. It does not check travel time, capacity, equipment, aliases, time-zone rules or calendars. The date is a shared label you provide. DST skipped or repeated clock times are unsupported and are not detected automatically.

Will my agenda be uploaded or saved?

The application does not transmit your agenda, use analytics or write browser storage. Loading static website files initially still needs a network connection. Inputs stay in this page and clear on leaving. The page does not delete your browser history or downloaded files.
