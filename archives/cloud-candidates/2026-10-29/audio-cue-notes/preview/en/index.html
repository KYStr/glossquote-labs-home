    How it works

    Does this recognize speech?

    No. You listen, mark and write highlights manually. There is no AI, recording or automatic transcription.

    Can I save my progress?

    There is no autosave or JSON import in this version. Download TXT or JSON before leaving. Switching languages leaves and clears the page. Downloaded files are managed by you and your browser.

    What happens when I replace audio?

    After you confirm, old notes are cleared only when the new file loads successfully. If it cannot load, your existing notes remain. Clear all immediately releases the audio and notes.
