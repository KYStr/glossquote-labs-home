Three things to know before you plan

Does the latest start guarantee I can finish?

No. These are theoretical latest dates based on your calendar and dependencies. Staffing, equipment, buffers, an actual start date and whether today is too late are not checked. Parallel tasks may overlap.

How are durations and deadlines counted?

Durations include the first and last workdays. A successor may start no earlier than the next workday after its predecessor finishes. If the deadline is not a workday, terminal tasks finish on the previous workday.

Are my inputs saved?

Inputs are not uploaded, tracked or stored. The initial static page load still requires a network. Editing inputs invalidates the result. Leaving or hiding the page, switching language or reloading clears inputs and results. Copy and download run only when you press their buttons.
