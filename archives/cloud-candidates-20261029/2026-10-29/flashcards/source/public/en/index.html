How it works & common questions

What CSV format is accepted?

Use exactly the two-column header question,answer. No trimming, case changes or Unicode normalization. CRLF/CR become LF; one leading BOM is accepted. Quoted fields can contain commas, newlines and doubled quotes. One final record-ending newline is allowed; other blank records are rejected.

What happens to spaces, duplicates and special characters?

Edge spaces and duplicate cards stay intact. Every card has its own source ID, but neither face may be entirely whitespace. Unpaired surrogates and C0/C1 controls are rejected, except Tab and line feed. HTML and script-looking text stay plain text. Code points are not visual character counts.

Does the tool automatically grade my answers?

No. After revealing the answer, choose Practice again or Remembered. You can change that rating; only the latest rating per card is counted. The tool does not move automatically or schedule spaced repetition. Changing cards hides the answer.

What clears my practice?

Editing CSV, changing shuffle, beginning IME composition, loading a sample or restarting clears the previous practice and ratings. Leaving or changing language clears the page too. There is no history, account or sync. Cancel or Escape in the clear confirmation keeps your work.

Can I keep practicing after an export?

Downloading leaves the current practice intact. Up to four started downloads keep temporary data for 60 seconds, even after editing or clearing; leaving the page releases them immediately. JSON saves readable questions and progress, but this version cannot import it again. TXT includes all answers, without crop marks or duplex alignment; choose your layout in a text editor. Exporting does not upload or share your file.

Is there no network traffic at all?

No. Loading the site still requests HTML, CSS and scripts from its host; question processing has no upload API. Reopening offline is not guaranteed. The page cannot delete downloaded files or erase browser internals. This is an unpublished noindex preview, not a live release or comprehensive security certification.
