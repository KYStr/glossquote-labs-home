LOCAL LIST UTILITY / VCARD 3.0

CSV to Contact Cards

A small list. Ready for your address book.

Map your CSV name, phone and email columns to vCard 3.0. Review the cards before downloading a VCF for manual import.

Processed in this page’s memory. No contact access, uploads, accounts or content storage. The downloaded VCF is unencrypted; keep it private.

CSV to Contact Cards

1 · Bring your list

Comma-separated UTF-8 only; LF or CRLF record endings. Quoted commas, escaped quotes and multiline cells are supported. No automatic number or formula conversion.

Limits: 256 KiB input · 500 contacts plus one header · 20 columns · 4096 UTF-16 units per cell · 512 KiB VCF.

2 · Match columns

Choose by position; duplicate header names remain distinct. Map a phone or email (or both). Every contact must have a name and at least one channel. Nothing is silently skipped or deduplicated.

JavaScript and modern browser features are required. This candidate has not been tested on your device.

What will be exported?

vCard 3.0 only: FN, N, and optional TEL / EMAIL. Full names are not split: the complete name goes in the given-name component of N; surname stays empty. Sorting and display can differ between contacts apps. Phone + and leading zeros remain text.

Names: 200 code points; newline becomes a vCard escaped newline. Phone: 80 characters, digits, spaces, ( ) . -, and optional leading +; no extensions. Email: one common ASCII address up to 254 characters; no display names or internationalized addresses. No verification of ownership, delivery or number validity.

Prepare for manual import only. This tool never opens your contacts app, emails anyone or uploads your list. It does not protect spreadsheet exports because no CSV export is provided. No device import compatibility is claimed.
