    Filename rules

    Each full output name may use up to 180 UTF-8 bytes; some characters use multiple bytes. Path separators, control characters, disallowed Windows characters, and reserved device names are rejected. Names cannot end in an ASCII space or dot. Original extensions are kept: .gitignore has no extension, while .env.local keeps .local.

    Frequently asked questions

    Will this change or overwrite my originals?

    No. The tool downloads a separate ZIP copy. Your browser or OS controls the download location and same-name behavior.

    Does the ZIP compress photos or documents?

    No. It uses the ZIP STORE method and does not compress files. Unzip the download before use.

    Does it remove photo EXIF or document metadata?

    No. It preserves the original bytes and does not inspect or remove embedded metadata.

    Why are some names rejected?

    Unsafe names, reserved names, or collisions stop the whole archive so names are not silently altered.
