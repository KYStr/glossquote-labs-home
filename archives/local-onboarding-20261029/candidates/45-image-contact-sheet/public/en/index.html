    Format, size and processing limits

    Static JPEG / PNG only, after file header and browser decoding checks. APNG, GIF, SVG, HEIC, unknown and damaged formats are rejected. The header determines the actual format.

    Up to 30 images, 8 MiB each and 64 MiB total. Each image is limited to 16 MP and 8,192 px on either edge. The output is limited to 16 MP, 8,192 px per edge, 64 MiB PNG and 128 KiB TXT. Large photos can still fail if the browser runs out of memory.

    Images are scaled proportionally and centered without cropping. Transparent areas become white. Filename labels show up to 32 Unicode characters on two lines; the TXT keeps full names. Files are processed in this page's memory without browser storage. The export does not carry source location or camera metadata.

    Stopping waits for the current image operation to finish. Clearing or leaving releases the data this page holds. Downloads already handed to your browser cannot be recalled; originals and downloaded files on your computer are not deleted.

    Are photos cropped?

    No. Each photo is scaled proportionally and centered with blank space where needed. Unused cells in the last row also stay white.

    How do numbers map to filenames?

    The PNG always shows numbers and can also show shortened filenames. Each TXT row lists the number, full filename and visual pixel dimensions. Filenames are JSON strings: newlines and tabs appear as escaped text and cannot create extra entries.
