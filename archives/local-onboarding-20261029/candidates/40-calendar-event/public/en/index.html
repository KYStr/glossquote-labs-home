From event details to your calendar

  1. Enter a title, complete dates and times in your selected zone, or choose all day.
  2. Preview the local times. If a time occurs twice, explicitly choose the first or second occurrence.
  3. Download the ICS file, import it into your calendar, and check the displayed dates and times.

Details stay in this page’s memory. Leaving or clearing removes the draft; it does not delete files you have already downloaded.

Common questions

Does this add the event to my calendar?

No. It creates an ICS file for you to import. Import steps and display can differ between calendars; check the event when importing.

How do time zones work?

Timed events are resolved in your selected zone and saved in UTC. Your calendar may display its own local time. Repeated times require a choice; skipped times are rejected.

Is the last all-day date included?

Yes. The file uses the following date as its exclusive end, as required by ICS.

Are my details uploaded?

Form data and the file stay in page memory. There is no account or sync; drafts are not retained after leaving. Language links do not include your input.
