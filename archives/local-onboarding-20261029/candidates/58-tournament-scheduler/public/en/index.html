      Before you schedule

      What happens when there is an odd number of teams?

      Each round has one bye; in a single round robin, every team receives one bye.

      Will limited courts remove any matches?

      No. Each round is split into additional slots in a fixed order, and all matchups remain in the schedule.

      Does the home/away label mean a real home venue?

      No. The second meeting in a double round robin swaps the labels. The tool does not know the actual venue or advantage.
