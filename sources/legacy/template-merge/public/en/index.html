Data stays in this page
Your list, template and results stay in page memory, with no upload or saved history. Clearing, changing language or leaving releases this page’s references. It does not remove downloaded files or securely erase memory.

      
Supported format and limits
UTF-8 CSV only. Commas, newlines and double quotes inside cells must follow CSV quoting rules. Record separators must consistently use CRLF, LF or CR. Templates are limited to 32,768 UTF-16 code units; complete TXT and JSON files together are limited to 8 MiB.

      
Common questions
Does the template support conditions?
No. It performs one literal substitution for each {{column}} field.


Will messages be sent automatically?
No. You review, copy or download the text yourself.


What happens to blank cells?
Generation is blocked by default and the missing rows are listed. You may explicitly allow blanks to become empty text.
