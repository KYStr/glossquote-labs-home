    How to use

    Enter a start time, activity names, and whole-minute durations. Notes are optional. Use Move up and Move down to change the order, then select Build timeline. Any edit immediately clears the previous result.

    No calendar dates, time zones, daylight-saving rules, or reminders are used. Changing language leaves this page and clears the data. A clipboard write already accepted by the browser cannot be recalled. You and your browser manage the clipboard and downloaded files.

    Frequently asked questions

    Is my timeline saved?

    No. Inputs and results stay in this page’s memory and are cleared when you leave.

    What happens when the schedule passes midnight?

    Times after midnight are marked “next day.” No calendar date, time zone, or daylight-saving rules are used.

    Can I build a timeline without a target end time?

    Yes. Leave it blank and keep “Target is the next day” unchecked; no early/late comparison is shown.

    How long can each activity be?

    Each activity can be 1 to 720 minutes. The full timeline is limited to 1440 minutes and 60 activities.
