How to use

Choose zero or two decimal places. One person uses this page to collect everyone's shared costs. Add up what each person paid, including anyone who paid nothing with 0. Both modes total these payments automatically and list who pays whom. You never need to enter the total separately. Quick settlement splits equally. Advanced split offers equal splitting or custom shares. A person with zero shares owes nothing but can still pay upfront; shares cannot all be zero. All-zero payments are valid. Names, payments, and precision are shared between modes, and advanced share drafts are kept. Switching or editing clears the old result, so calculate again before exporting. This is not a per-expense ledger or a split for different subgroups. It supports 2 to 50 people, uses one currency, runs locally, and keeps no history. It does not send money.

How is rounding handled?

Smallest units go to the largest remainders first. Ties follow creation order. Shares always add up to the exact bill total.

Does this send money?

No. It only calculates reproducible suggestions. It does not send money, reminders, or connect accounts, and does not claim to minimize transfer count.

Can I mix currencies?

No currency conversion is available. Choose zero or two decimals for one currency and check that the precision suits your unit.

How do quick and advanced modes differ?

Both modes total each person's payments automatically and suggest transfers. Quick settlement splits equally. Advanced split offers equal splitting or custom shares. A person with zero shares owes nothing, but their payment still counts toward what they should receive.
