Calculation method and limits

Target units = observed use × (lead days + coverage days) ÷ observation days + safety reserve. Subtract current stock, then round up to whole packs, with a minimum of zero packs. Decisions use exact integer ratios. Daily use is displayed to three decimal places with half-up rounding.

Shortage before arrival excludes the new order. Whole days of stock use current stock and fixed average use, without deducting reserve. Runway is not estimated at zero use. This version does not handle incoming orders, multiple warehouses, unit conversion or changing demand. It is an estimate, not a demand forecast.

Common questions

How does the planner estimate the number of packs?

It estimates a fixed average from observed use, includes lead time, desired coverage after arrival and safety reserve, subtracts current stock, then rounds up to whole packs. It is an estimate from your inputs, not a demand forecast.

Can it calculate when observed use is zero?

Yes. Stock runway is not estimated at zero use. Safety reserve still counts toward the target, so an order may still be suggested.

Are incoming orders included in current stock?

No. This version uses current stock only and does not handle incoming orders or multiple warehouses. A changing usage rate may make the fixed-average estimate inaccurate.
