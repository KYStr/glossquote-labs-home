Does this planner guarantee the fewest stock sticks?

No. It uses a fixed FFD heuristic to produce a repeatable estimate; it does not guarantee a minimum or optimal plan.

How is kerf counted?

Only once between adjacent pieces on the same stick. No kerf is deducted before the first piece, between the last piece and remaining stock, or for the saw cuts that remove end trim.

Does remaining length include end trim, and can I reuse it?

No. It is the unallocated usable length after pieces and between-piece kerf; end trim is listed separately. Reuse is not guaranteed, so check the remainder on site.

Does the planner account for defects, clamping or the real cutting method?

No. Those shop conditions are outside this model and need to be checked separately.
