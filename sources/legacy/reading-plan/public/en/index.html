    Planning and your data

    How are the remaining pages split?

    The tool counts selected weekdays inside your date range and divides the remaining pages evenly. Earlier reading days receive the extra pages first. If fewer pages remain, later available dates become flexible / rest days.

    How do I update a plan after falling behind?

    Change the completed page count and your new start date, then recalculate. The tool does not track reading or automatically move the start date to today.

    Does it skip public holidays?

    No. It uses only the weekdays you select and does not check local holidays or school calendars. Adjust your dates and selected weekdays yourself.

    Are my book details saved or uploaded?

    No account or cloud storage is used. Data stays in this tab and is cleared when you leave or reload. Copy and download happen only after you choose those actions. The initial load still downloads website files. Your browser manages copied or downloaded content.
