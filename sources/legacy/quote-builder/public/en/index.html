Before sharing

Does this verify my tax rate?

No. You enter the rate; the tool only calculates from that value and does not determine whether it applies.

Is the download an invoice?

No. The HTML and JSON are quote data you entered. They do not provide an invoice, signature, or legal validity check.

Can I download the old quote after editing?

No. Editing invalidates the preview and its download links. Create a new preview before downloading again.
