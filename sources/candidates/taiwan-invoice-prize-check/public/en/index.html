Taiwan Uniform Invoice Prize Check

Copy this period's winning numbers from the official Ministry of Finance announcement into the setup below, then check your invoices. Everything runs locally in your browser — nothing is uploaded or stored. This tool checks the numbers you typed against the standard prize amounts; the official announcement for the period governs, and a typing error in the winning numbers produces wrong verdicts, so double-check the setup after entering it. Full-width digits (０-９) are converted to ASCII automatically.
