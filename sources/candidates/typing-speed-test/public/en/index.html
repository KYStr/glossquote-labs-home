Typing Speed Test

Choose a duration and a passage language, then type the passage character by character. The timer starts at the first committed character; the input locks at expiry or at completion and the score is computed by the fixed formulas below. Everything runs on your device: typed text is never uploaded or stored, and reloading discards the attempt.

This test is scored for physical keyboards; touch keyboards are not a scored environment. Please take it on a desktop or laptop.

Scoring at a glance: Net WPM = correct characters ÷ 5 ÷ minutes; Chinese passages score net CPM (correct characters ÷ minutes) instead. Full rules at the foot of the page.

    The full scoring rules
    • Comparison is strictly positional: position i is correct if and only if the typed character at i equals the passage character at i (compared as Unicode code points). A missed character makes every later position wrong until you backspace and fix the string.
    • The timer starts at the first committed character; IME composition in progress is not scored and not displayed — only committed characters count.
    • At expiry the full chosen duration is used; at completion the actual elapsed time, to one decimal, is used.
    • English: gross WPM = total characters ÷ 5 ÷ minutes; net WPM = correct characters ÷ 5 ÷ minutes. Chinese: gross/net CPM = total / correct characters ÷ minutes, and the headline says CPM only.
    • Speeds round to the nearest integer, halves away from zero; accuracy = correct ÷ total, shown to one decimal.
    • Paste is refused and characters beyond the passage length are refused. The attempt exists in memory only; reload or navigation discards it.
