Metronome

Every click is computed from this device's audio clock and scheduled ahead, so timing does not depend on how busy the main thread is. Timing is generated by the audio clock; in some browsers, sound may be paused while the tab is hidden, and this tool does not claim background playback.

How it works, and its limits
  • Scheduling is lookahead-based: every 25 ms the scheduler places clicks for the next 0.12 s on the audio clock. Event times accumulate on the audio clock only; timer jitter never enters the beat grid.
  • Clicks are synthesised oscillators (accent 1568 Hz, beat 1046 Hz, subdivision 784 Hz, each under 50 ms); there are no audio files.
  • Tap tempo: taps within 2.0 s of each other form a run; BPM is the rounded mean of the last up to 6 intervals, clamped to 30-240.
  • Changing tempo, signature or subdivision while playing affects only events not yet scheduled; clicks already inside the horizon keep their times.
  • Settings live in memory only; reloading restores the defaults (100 BPM, 4/4, subdivision 1, accent on, volume 80%).
