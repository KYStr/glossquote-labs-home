Word and Character Frequency Counter

Paste text and get three ranked tables: Words (tokens of letters and digits outside the CJK classes — Greek, Cyrillic and every other script included), Characters (Han, kana and Hangul counted one by one), and Bigrams (adjacent Han pairs). This tool does not segment Chinese — segmentation is a research problem; characters and bigrams keep every number reproducible by hand from the stated rules. Word matching uses Unicode simple case folding, not ordinary lowercasing.

Counting rules (shown beside the results, so every number can be rechecked)
  • Classes: Han (U+4E00–U+9FFF, U+3400–U+4DBF), kana (U+3040–U+30FF) and Hangul (U+AC00–U+D7AF) count per character; every other Unicode letter or digit forms words — so Greek, Cyrillic, Arabic and other scripts land in the Words table. It is not a Latin-only table.
  • Words: an apostrophe joins only with a word character on both sides (don't is one token; 'tis counts as tis); hyphens and all other punctuation separate. Matching folds with Unicode simple case folding (the simple case folding entries — C and S — of CaseFolding.txt, Unicode 16.0.0): Σ and ς both fold to σ — unlike lowercasing, under which ς stays ς. The display form is the most frequent original casing, ties going to the first occurrence.
  • Bigrams: adjacent pairs inside a Han run only; punctuation ends a run; kana and Hangul form no bigrams. The Characters table does not normalise: visually identical text in different Unicode normalisation forms can count differently.
  • Stopwords apply after tokenisation and before totals, so totals are after filters; a bigram is filtered when either character is a stopword character. Ordering is count descending, then first-occurrence position; percentages are shares of the table's filtered total, one decimal, rounded half away from zero.
  • Default stopwords (English): a, about, above, after, again, against, all, am, an, and, any, are, as, at, be, because, been, before, being, below, between, both, but, by, could, did, do, does, doing, down, during, each, few, for, from, further, had, has, have, having, he, her, here, hers, herself, him, himself, his, how, i, if, in, into, is, it, its, itself, just, me, more, most, my, no, nor, not, now, of, off, on, once, only, or, other, our, ours, out, over, own, same, she, should, so, some, such, than, that, the, their, theirs, them, themselves, then, there, these, they, this, those, through, to, too, under, until, up, very, was, we, were, what, when, where, which, while, who, whom, why, will, with, you, your, yours, yourself
