Cron Expression Explainer

This tool speaks standard five-field Vixie/POSIX cron (minute, hour, day of month, month, day of week). Paste an expression to get a per-field explanation, a plain-language summary of the whole schedule, and the next ten firing times in your local timezone. Quartz dialect tokens (?, L, W, #), seconds/year fields and @reboot are out of scope — they produce a named error, never a silent misreading.

    How the rules work

    • The DOM/DOW OR rule: when both day fields are restricted (neither starts with *), the job runs when EITHER matches — the most common cron surprise, and the explanation calls it out explicitly.
    • What a/n means: 5/15 is 5-59/15 (from a to the maximum, every n), not "only at a".
    • In the weekday field, both 0 and 7 are Sunday; names (JAN–DEC, SUN–SAT) are case-insensitive.
    • Supported macros: @hourly, @daily, @midnight, @weekly, @monthly, @yearly, @annually — entered macros expand visibly into fields.
    • A local time that does not exist because of a DST transition (such as 02:30 on a spring-forward day) never fires; enumeration follows platform time semantics without compensation.
    • When nothing fires, the tool says so: the search window is four years plus a day (covering the leap-day cycle), and a schedule like February 31st never fires.
