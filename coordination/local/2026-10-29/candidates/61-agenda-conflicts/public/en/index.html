        Scope and data handling

        Check 1–200 sessions, up to 20 rooms and 100 speakers, within one local civil day and time zone. Every session pair is compared. The tool does not reschedule sessions, estimate walking distance, or assign staff. Midnight crossings, time zone conversions, and daylight saving changes are not supported.

        Input and reports stay in this page’s memory. They are not uploaded, saved to browser storage, or put in the URL. A local file is created only when you press Download. Closing, leaving, or switching language clears the page, so download first. Clearing cannot remove downloaded files or securely erase memory.

        Frequently asked questions

        Do back-to-back sessions count as overlapping?

        No. If one ends at 10:00 and the next starts at 10:00, their half-open intervals do not share a minute.

        What happens when a room or speaker is blank?

        It is marked unassigned. Conflicts for that resource cannot be determined; the tool does not report it as conflict-free.

        Will a transition warning duplicate a speaker clash?

        No. An overlapping pair appears once with all error reasons. A transition warning is only for a non-overlapping pair with a shared speaker and a gap below the configured threshold.
