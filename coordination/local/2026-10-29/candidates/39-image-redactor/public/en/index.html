    How to use this tool

    The support targets are current stable desktop Chrome and Edge. Mobile preview is best effort. If your device cannot process the image, try a desktop browser or a smaller image. The tool will not resize or upload it.

    Does the tool find emails or order numbers automatically?

    No. Draw every area you want to cover.

    Can I move a mask after downloading?

    No. The exported PNG contains composited pixels and no editable mask layer. Your source image remains on your device.

    Is my image uploaded?

    Your image is processed in the current browser page, with no upload or storage feature. Clearing the image or leaving releases references held by the program. Browser memory cleanup is not secure erasure and does not delete the source on your device.

    Why do transparent areas become white?

    The export composites the image over white before drawing black masks, so viewers do not choose different transparent backgrounds.

    Does this guarantee a safely anonymized image?

    No. It covers only the rectangles you choose. Inspect the whole export and its unmasked areas.
