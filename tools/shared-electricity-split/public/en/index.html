How the split is calculated

When a total meter delta is provided, common-area usage is the total meter delta minus the sum of the room submeters. It is shared equally across rooms first, then added to each room's own usage to form its weight. Amounts are allocated by weight with the largest remainder method: each room first gets its whole-dollar floor, and the remaining dollars go one each to the largest remainders, ties broken by input order, so the room amounts always sum exactly to the total bill. Without a total meter delta, the bill is split by room usage only. If every room used 0 kWh and there is no common usage, the bill is split equally instead.

Shares are shown to one decimal place, and the common-share and billable readings are each rounded independently, so displayed values may not add up to the total meter delta or exactly 100.0%; amounts are always computed from the exact weights, never from the displayed readings. Room names are NFC-normalized and trimmed of leading and trailing spaces, at most 24 characters.

The downloaded report is always named shared-electricity-split-report.json. It contains the result as compact JSON with keys sorted recursively by UTF-16 code units and one trailing newline.

Frequently asked questions

How is common-area usage handled?
The total meter delta minus the sum of the room submeters is split equally across rooms first, then added to each room's own usage before the proportional split.
Why don't the shares add up to 100%?
Shares are shown to one decimal place and may not total exactly 100% after rounding; the amounts are allocated by the largest remainder method and always sum exactly to the bill.
