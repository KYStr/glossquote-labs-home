How it works and limits

  • Two formulas side by side: Epley and Brzycki model the reps-to-weight relationship differently; the headline result is their average.
  • At 1 rep, the weight you lifted is the 1RM itself, so both formulas return the input weight.
  • Output loads are always in kilograms, in 0.5 kg steps so they match plates you can actually load; the percentage table is based on the rounded estimated 1RM.
  • An estimate is not a measurement: accuracy drops as reps rise, and maximal attempts need safeties and a spotter.
  • Input stays in this page only — nothing is uploaded or stored, and leaving the page discards it.

Why do the two formulas differ?

They model the reps-to-weight relationship differently; this tool shows both and uses their average as the headline result.

Are more reps more accurate?

No. Past about 10 reps the estimates lose accuracy noticeably.

Why are loads in 0.5 kg steps?

Gym plates adjust in 0.5 kg steps, so results match loads you can actually set.
