How it is calculated, and where the yields come from

Volumes come from the centimetre dimensions: slabs and strip footings are length × width × thickness (depth); cylinders are π × (diameter/2)² × height, with π fixed to 30 decimal places. The net volume is multiplied by the count, then the waste allowance is added once to give the adjusted volume; each bag count is the unrounded adjusted volume divided by the per-bag yield, rounded up — the displayed 3-decimal volumes are never used to derive bag counts. All arithmetic is exact rational arithmetic with no floating point.

Per-bag yields are fixed from major premix manufacturers' published product data: a 40 lb bag yields about 0.30 ft³ (0.0084950539776 m³), a 60 lb bag about 0.45 ft³ (0.0127425809664 m³), and an 80 lb bag about 0.60 ft³ (0.0169901079552 m³), converted with 1 ft³ = 0.028316846592 m³. Results are shown on screen only — there is no download or export — and the text can be selected manually.

Frequently asked questions

Where is the waste allowance applied?
It is added once to the total volume before converting to bags; the bag counts get no second allowance.
Are the yields guaranteed?
No. They are a purchasing estimate converted from the manufacturers' published per-bag yields; site grading and thickness variation change actual usage.
