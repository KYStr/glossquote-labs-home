Plates per side and achievable weight

本工具只依目標總重選槓片，不估算任何人的最大重量，也不把結果當訓練處方或安全保證。全部計算都在這個頁面本機進行，不上傳、不儲存、不記錄你的輸入。

This tool only picks plates for a target total. It never estimates anyone's maximum lift, and its result is not a training prescription or a safety guarantee. All calculation happens locally on this page; nothing is uploaded, stored, or logged.

How plates are chosen

Weight per side is (target total − bar weight) ÷ 2. Plates are loaded greedily from largest to smallest: 25, 20, 15, 10, 5, 2.5, 1.25 kg, at most the pairs you have of each. All weights are computed in integer units of 0.125 kg, with no floating point. Collars count as 0 kg; micro plates (0.5 kg and below) are not in this set.

When the target is not reachable, the per-side remainder is above 0, and the page shows the achievable total and the next heavier suggestion; that suggestion is “one more plate of the smallest denomination still spare”, not a proven global minimum achievable weight.

All calculation happens locally on this page. Nothing is uploaded, stored, or logged.

Frequently asked questions

What does “not reachable” mean?
A per-side remainder above 0 means your plates cannot make the target; the page shows the achievable total and the next heavier suggestion.
Does it estimate my one-rep max (1RM)?
No. It only picks plates for a target total; it never estimates anyone's maximum lift.
Are collar weights included?
No. Collars count as 0 kg; if yours have weight, add it to the bar weight field.
