How it is calculated, and limits

Weight per metre = cross-section (π × radius²) × material density: 1.75 mm filament has a cross-section of about 2.405 mm², one metre of it has a volume equal to that many cm³, and multiplying by the density (g/cm³) gives grams per metre. Length-to-weight, weight-to-length, spool remaining and cost all derive from that figure, with no rounding until the single display rounding.

Spool remaining = gross weight − empty spool weight, divided by the weight per metre for the remaining length; print cost = spool price × filament used ÷ spool net weight (a linear estimate), with the currency shown as a label only. This tool does not read G-code or slicer files, does not estimate supports, skirts or flow differences, and does not guarantee a slicer's usage figure.

FAQ

Why does my slicer's weight differ?
Slicers compute from actual extrusion paths and flow; this tool converts from nominal diameter and density, so small differences are expected.
How do I get the empty spool weight?
Weigh a finished spool of the same kind or check the maker's published value; it can vary between batches.
