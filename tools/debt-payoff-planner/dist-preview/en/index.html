          How it calculates, and the fixed rules

          Each month, interest is charged first on the opening balance (whole cents, half-up), then minimum payments are made in input order, and finally the extra monthly payment, the minimum payments freed by debts paid off from the next month on, and any unused part of this month's minimums are paid in strategy order, one debt at a time. Snowball pays the smallest balance first; avalanche pays the highest APR first; the order is fixed once at the start of the plan and never re-sorted. Total monthly outflow is fixed at all minimum payments plus the extra monthly payment, except for the final month, when the remaining balance is smaller. If a plan cannot clear everything within 600 months, the tool says so honestly and shows the remaining balance instead of inventing a payoff date.

          Frequently asked questions

          What is the difference between snowball and avalanche?
          Snowball pays the smallest balance first; avalanche pays the highest APR first. Avalanche usually costs less interest; snowball usually clears one debt sooner.
          What happens to a paid-off debt's minimum payment?
          From the next month it joins the extra-payment pool and rolls to the next debt; the total monthly outflow stays the same.
          What if a minimum payment is less than the interest?
          That balance grows; if it cannot be cleared within 600 months, the tool says so and shows the remaining balance.
          Does it connect to my bank?
          No. All calculations run on this page and nothing is uploaded.
