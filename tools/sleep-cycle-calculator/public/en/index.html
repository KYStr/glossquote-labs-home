Sleep Cycle Calculator

One sleep cycle is counted as 90 minutes. Enter the time you need to wake up and get four bedtimes, or enter a bedtime and get four wake times. The time you need to fall asleep is added on its own — never quietly absorbed into the first cycle.

One sleep cycle averages about 90 minutes, but cycle length varies from person to person and from night to night; 90 minutes is only the conventional planning average. The suggestions here are planning estimates worked out from the time you enter — not medical advice — and cannot guarantee you will wake in any particular sleep stage. Most adults are commonly advised to get 7–9 hours of sleep, but individual needs vary.
