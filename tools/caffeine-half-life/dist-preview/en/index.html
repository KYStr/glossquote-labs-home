    How it works and limits

    • Each entry decays exponentially from its intake moment with the same half-life: after t minutes, mg × 2^(−t / half-life minutes) remains. An entry later than the query time contributes nothing (no wrap to the previous day).
    • The total is rounded to one decimal after summing the unrounded per-entry contributions; each entry is rounded separately, so the displayed total can differ from the sum of the displayed entries by 0.1 mg.
    • Drink presets are a fixed table: Espresso (30 ml) 63, Brewed coffee (240 ml) 95, Black tea (240 ml) 47, Green tea (240 ml) 28, Cola (355 ml) 34, Energy drink (250 ml) 80.
    • Single-day clock model only: no absorption delay, no multi-day tracking, no sleep prediction. Data stays in this page — nothing is uploaded or stored, and leaving the page discards it. This tool has no download or export.

    What if the query time is earlier than a drink?

    It contributes nothing before its intake time; the hourly series starts counting it once its time passes.

    Can this predict my sleep?

    No. It is a decay estimate with a single half-life; individual metabolism varies widely.
