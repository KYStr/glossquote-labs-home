Results are static estimates, not a generation guarantee, a quote, or investment advice. Actual generation depends on orientation, tilt, shading, climate, and equipment; the applicable tariff is the one announced for the period when the system is completed and grid-connected, per the Taipower contract.

How it is calculated and the fixed rules

Annual generation = system capacity × annual effective sun hours. Self-used kWh follow the self-use ratio; export revenue = exported kWh × feed-in tariff, self-use saving = self-used kWh × retail price; payback years = installation cost ÷ annual benefit. The 20-year total benefit = annual benefit × 20. Module degradation, maintenance cost, discounting, inflation, subsidies, and future tariff changes are not counted.

Feed-in tariff table (2026 rooftop rates, MOEA announcement dated 2 January 2026, Ref. No. 11458005630): 1 kW to below 10 kW, NT$5.6279/kWh; 10 kW to below 20 kW, NT$5.3819/kWh; 20 kW to below 50 kW, NT$4.2505/kWh; 50 kW to below 100 kW, NT$4.0459/kWh; 100 kW to below 500 kW, NT$3.7152/kWh; 500 kW and above, NT$3.6236/kWh. Band lower bounds are inclusive, upper bounds exclusive. The 15% northern-region bonus, the 6% high-efficiency module bonus, and local government subsidies are all excluded; ground-mounted and water-surface rates are not used by this tool.

Roof rule: each 1 kWp needs 8 m² (a planning value including module spacing and maintenance access). A shortfall is a warning, not an error, and the page shows the largest capacity that fits. Ping conversion is display only: 1 ping = 400/121 m², shown to 2 decimal places.

Frequently asked questions

Which year's feed-in tariff is used?
The 2026 (ROC 115) MOEA-announced rooftop rates, identical in both phases; the rate that applies is the one announced for the period when the system is completed and grid-connected, per the Taipower contract.
How is self-consumed electricity valued?
Self-used kWh are valued at the retail price you enter (default NT$3.00/kWh is an assumption — replace it with the marginal rate on your own bill; Taipower residential rates are inclining-block, which this tool does not compute).
What if my roof is too small?
That is a warning, not an error; the page shows the largest capacity that fits, so you can lower the system size and recalculate.
