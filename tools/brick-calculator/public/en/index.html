How it is calculated

Net bricks are the net area divided by the effective brick face (including the joint), rounded up. Bricks to buy add waste to the unrounded net count first, then round up — never to the already-rounded integer. Mortar volume is wall volume minus brick volume. Preset sizes are commonly used nominal manufacturer sizes (Taiwan red brick 230 × 60 × 110 mm, UK standard brick 215 × 65 × 102.5 mm, US modular brick 194 × 57 × 92 mm); actual supplied sizes follow the supplier.

All calculation happens locally on this page. Nothing is uploaded, stored, or recorded. The result covers one single-leaf wall only.

Frequently asked questions

Where is waste applied?
Waste is added to the unrounded net brick count, then rounded up — never to the already-rounded integer.
Why is mortar zero with a 0 mm joint?
Mortar here is wall volume minus brick volume, so a zero joint leaves no mortar space.
Is this a structural design?
No. This is a purchasing estimate, not a structural design; follow professional design for load-bearing, mix and workmanship.
