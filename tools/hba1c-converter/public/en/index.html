Frequently asked questions

Is eAG a measured average glucose?

No. eAG is estimated from A1c by regression over roughly the past 2–3 months; it is not a measured value.

Why does mmol/L sometimes differ from the ADA table by 0.1?

This tool uses the ADAG paper's mmol/L regression (1.5944 × A1c − 2.594). The ADA table's rows at A1c 8.0% and 8.5% cannot be reproduced to 1 decimal by any published formula (this tool gives 10.2 / 11.0; the table gives 10.1 / 10.9); the other 7 rows agree.

What A1c should I aim for?

ADA suggests <7.0% as a general goal for most nonpregnant adults; set your personal goal with your clinician.

Can this diagnose diabetes?

No. Diagnosis requires laboratory testing and confirmation by a clinician.

Conversion happens on this device; nothing is sent or stored. Formulas: eAG (mg/dL) = 28.7 × A1c − 46.7 (ADAG / Nathan 2008); eAG (mmol/L) = 1.5944 × A1c − 2.594; IFCC (mmol/mol) = (A1c% − 2.15) × 10.929. Category cut points follow the ADA diagnostic criteria.
