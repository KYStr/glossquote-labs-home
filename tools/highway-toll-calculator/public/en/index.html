    How it is calculated

    Rates (per km, normal days and regular holidays): the first 20 km of the day are free; 20–200 km cost 1.20 for small vehicles, 1.50 for large vehicles, and 1.80 for articulated vehicles; beyond 200 km the rates are 0.90, 1.12, and 1.35 (25% off). The large-vehicle long-distance rate of 1.12 per km is the official table value.

    The 20 km allowance and the 200 km threshold are settled per vehicle per day: all of the day's distance is combined and the 20 km are deducted only once. This trip's charge is gross (distance already driven today + this trip's distance) − gross (distance already driven today). eTag takes 10% off first, then the result is rounded half-up to whole dollars; rounding happens only once, at the end.

    In entry & exit mode the distance is the difference between the two interchanges' distances from the Keelung end (Freeway 1, 14 interchanges, Keelung End 0.0 km to Kaohsiung End 372.8 km).

    Frequently asked questions

    Is the 20 km free allowance per trip?

    No — once per vehicle per day; all trips on the same day are settled together by licence plate.

    How do I get the 10% eTag discount?

    Keep a prepaid eTag balance high enough to cover the toll, or pay by yourself within 3–5 days after passage; the discount applies before rounding to the nearest dollar.

    Does this apply on long holidays?

    No. Holidays use a separately announced flat rate with no free kilometres or long-distance discount; this tool covers normal and regular weekend rates only.

    Why does the estimate differ from my actual bill by a few dollars?

    Actual bills sum per-gantry prices; this tool estimates from interchange distances. The amount charged by the toll operator is authoritative.
