How it is calculated

Each brokerage fee is the trade amount × fee rate × discount, rounded half-up to whole NT dollars, then compared with the per-order minimum fee — the minimum applies after the discount and after rounding. The sell side also pays securities transaction tax: trade amount × tax rate, rounded half-up to whole NT dollars, with no minimum and no discount. Cash day trades are taxed at 0.15%. For a partial sell, the cost basis of the sold shares is the total buy cost apportioned by share count, without rounding first; net profit or loss and return % are computed from the exact fraction and rounded only at the end. The breakeven sell price is the smallest sell price, in NT$0.01 steps, whose sell net reaches the sold cost basis under the same fee model.

The downloaded report is always named stock-profit-report.json. It is the result as compact JSON with object keys sorted by UTF-16 code units, two-decimal fields written with exactly two decimals, integer fields as integers, and one trailing line feed. All amounts are in New Taiwan dollars.

Frequently asked questions

Is the NT$20 minimum fee set by law?
No. Each broker sets its own per-order minimum, filed with the TWSE; NT$20 is the common round-lot convention and odd lots are often NT$1. Adjust it to your broker.
How do I enter my fee discount?
Enter the fraction you actually pay: 1 for none, 0.6 for 40% off. The discount applies to brokerage fees only, never to the transaction tax.
Is the 0.15% day-trading rate permanent?
It covers cash day trades only (same broker, account, day, stock and quantity) under Article 2-2 of the Securities Transaction Tax Act, in force through 31 Dec 2027; margin day trades stay at 0.3%.
Why does my broker app show a different figure?
Usual causes are how discounts settle (per trade or monthly rebate), fee rounding, or a different minimum fee. Match the parameters to your broker; the statement governs.
