Compound Interest & Savings Goal Calculator

The model used here: interest is compounded monthly at the nominal annual rate ÷ 12, each month's interest is rounded to the cent, and contributions land at month end. Results are a projection of that model, not a promise of any product's return. Every number comes from the month-by-month simulation — a continuous-compounding formula can differ by one cent (it never rounds monthly interest), and the simulation governs on this page.
