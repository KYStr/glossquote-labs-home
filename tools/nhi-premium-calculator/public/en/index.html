How it is calculated

The insured-amount table has 58 grades (NTD 29,500–313,000); the salary takes the smallest grade whose amount covers it. A salary above NTD 313,000 uses grade 58; below NTD 29,500 it uses grade 1. The premium rate is 5.17% and the burden shares are insured 30%, employer 60%, government 10%. The insured person's premium and the premium per dependent are the insured amount × 5.17% × 30%, rounded half up to whole NTD (exactly 0.5 rounds up), once only; the employee total is the per-person premium × (1 + dependents charged), with no second rounding. The employer and government shares use the announced average of 0.56 dependents — 1.56 persons in total — and do not depend on your actual number of dependents: the employer share is the insured amount × 5.17% × 60% × 1.56 and the government subsidy is the insured amount × 5.17% × 10% × 1.56, each rounded half up once at the end with no intermediate rounding. At most 3 dependents are charged. All amounts are whole New Taiwan dollars. The downloaded report is always named nhi-premium-report.json; its contents are the result as compact JSON with object keys sorted recursively by UTF-16 code units and one trailing line feed.

Frequently asked questions

What if I have more than three dependents?
Under Article 18 of the National Health Insurance Act, dependents beyond the first three are not charged.
Why doesn't the employer's share depend on my dependents?
The employer and government shares use the announced average of 0.56 dependents (1.56 persons in total), not your actual number of dependents.
Does this include the supplementary premium?
No. This tool estimates the regular premium only; the second-generation supplementary premium on bonuses and other income is out of scope.
Is this the amount the NHIA will bill?
It is an estimate from the NHIA tables effective January 1, 2026. Reported salary basis, part-time work and mid-month enrollment changes follow the NHIA's own determination.
