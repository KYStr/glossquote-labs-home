Input rules
  • Month and day are required; the year is optional. Each field accepts a decimal integer only (0, or starting with a non-zero digit; no spaces or fractions), at most 8 characters.
  • Ranges: month 1–12, day 1–31, year 1–9999; the date must also exist in its month.
  • February 29: accepted with no year given, accepted in a leap year, and reported as a nonexistent date in a non-leap year.
  • The date table is fixed and never varies by year or birth time; a birth date on a sign's first or last day adds a cusp note and the neighbouring sign.
  • The sign depends on month and day only; the year never changes the result.
Frequently asked questions

Born on Feb 29?
Pisces. The year only checks that the date exists; with no year given, Feb 29 is accepted.

Why the cusp note?
The exact ingress moment varies slightly by year; this tool uses a fixed date table and flags each sign's first and last day.
