How it works, and the assumptions

  • Gross wall area = 2 × (length + width) × wall height; paintable area = gross walls − openings + ceiling (when ticked).
  • A standard door is estimated at 0.9 × 2.1 m (1.89 m²) and a standard window at 1.5 × 1.2 m (1.80 m²); openings of any other size belong in the “other opening area” field.
  • Coverage is the paint manufacturer's figure for a standard surface; real coverage varies with surface texture and how the paint is applied.
  • Litres required = paintable area × coats ÷ coverage, computed exactly; the can count always rounds up to whole cans — never down, so you do not run out mid-wall.
  • The leftover is the paint remaining after buying whole cans, computed from the exact litres, so it can differ by 0.01 L from “total purchased − displayed litres required”.
