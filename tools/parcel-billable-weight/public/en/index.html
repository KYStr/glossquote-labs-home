Parcel Billable Weight

Measure each parcel's three sides (mm) and actual weight (g), then enter each service's divisor, gram step, minimum billable weight and optional limits, and press “Compare billable weights” to see each parcel's dimensional weight, billable weight and limit reasons per service.

Parcel measurements

1–12 parcels. One short name per parcel (1–24 characters, first character a letter or digit), three sides of 1–3000 mm each (any order; the tool finds the longest side), actual weight 1–100000 g.

Service rules

1–4 service rules of your own. Divisor and gram step are 1–100000 each, minimum billable weight is 0–100000 g (default 0); leave a limit blank for no limit.

The sample is fixed fake data (two parcels, services A and B; B's longest-side limit of 299 mm rejects parcel P); press “Compare billable weights” after loading to calculate.
