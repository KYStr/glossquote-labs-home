Credit Card Payoff Calculator

Enter the balance and APR from your statement to see how long a fixed monthly payment takes to clear the debt and how much interest it costs — or what you need to pay each month to finish within a target number of months. If a payment does not even cover the interest, the tool says so directly: that balance never pays off.

This is an estimate under the stated model. Your issuer's statement governs; fees, penalties, cash advances and minimum-payment rules are not modeled.

About this model

  • Interest is charged monthly on the opening balance; each payment covers interest first, then principal. All amounts are integer cents, with interest rounded half-up to the cent.
  • Target-months mode derives the payment from the exact annuity formula, rounded up to the cent, then verifies it with the same monthly simulation.
  • The simulation is capped at 600 months; if a plan cannot finish within the cap, the tool says so instead of printing a fake schedule.
  • This tool does not connect to a bank or import statements, and does not model minimum-payment rules, annual fees, penalties, cash advances or tiered rates.

FAQ

Why does it say the balance never pays off?

When the payment is no more than the first month's interest, the balance never shrinks.

How is the target-months payment derived?

From the exact annuity formula, rounded up to the cent, then verified by the same monthly simulation.
