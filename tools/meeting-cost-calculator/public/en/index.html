Meeting Cost Calculator

Meetings feel free because nobody multiplies a roomful of hourly rates by the time spent. Enter the attendees' headcounts and hourly rates (typed, or filled from an annual-salary preset) to see what a planned meeting burns; during the meeting, switch to the timer and watch the cost climb live.

Currency (symbol only, no conversion)

Attendees (up to five groups)

Five groups is the maximum.

The annual-salary presets are illustrative figures: hourly rate = annual salary ÷ 2080 (52 weeks × 40 hours), rounded to cents when filled in; after filling, the rate is an ordinary number you can edit. These figures are not anyone's actual salary.

Mid-meeting roster changes re-price as follows: cost = current rates × total elapsed time. Editing rates re-prices the whole elapsed time at the new rates; the tool does not track rate history.

Formulas and trade-offs

Burn = Σ (headcount × rate); total cost = burn × time ÷ 60 (manual mode: time in minutes; timer mode: elapsed milliseconds ÷ 3,600,000). Money is computed exactly and rounded half-up to cents once, at display time only — the live cost is always recomputed as current burn × total elapsed, never accumulated from per-tick rounded cents, so it cannot drift.

The currency selector changes the displayed symbol only; no exchange-rate conversion is performed. The figures are the price of the rates you entered, not anyone's true compensation cost; whether to fold taxes and benefits into the rates is your choice.
