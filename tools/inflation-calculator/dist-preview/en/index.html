Inflation Calculator

Convert an amount to another price level: project a future nominal amount and real purchasing power with a constant annual inflation rate, or convert an equivalent amount with two Consumer Price Index values.

Inputs

The same rate assumed every year, above −100% and at most 1000%, at most three decimal places. A negative value means deflation.

A whole number of years, 0–200.

The Consumer Price Index of the starting period, above 0 and at most 99,999.999, at most three decimal places.

The Consumer Price Index of the ending period, above 0 and at most 99,999.999, at most three decimal places. It may be lower than the starting index.

Constant-rate mode assumes the same inflation rate every year — a simplification. CPI mode results depend on the index source and periods you enter.

How it calculates, and the formats

Constant-rate mode: first the base 1 + annual rate ÷ 100, then the factor Math.pow(base, years); the future nominal amount is amount × factor, real purchasing power is amount ÷ factor, and cumulative inflation is (factor − 1) × 100%. Each year-table row k is recomputed independently with Math.pow(base, k), never by iterative multiplication. CPI mode: the ratio is ending index ÷ starting index; the converted amount is amount × ratio, the cumulative change is (ratio − 1) × 100%; when years between are given, the equivalent annual rate is (ratio raised to 1 ÷ years − 1) × 100%.

Amounts and cumulative percentages round to 2 decimal places, the equivalent annual rate to 4, each output rounded exactly once from the unrounded value. Negative values round half up toward positive infinity. When the factor underflows to 0, purchasing power shows “—” for beyond the computable range. The downloaded report is always named inflation-calculator-report.json; its content is the result as compact JSON with object keys sorted recursively by UTF-16 code units and one trailing line feed.

Frequently asked questions

What does constant-rate mode assume?
The same rate every year, compounded; real inflation varies year by year.
Where do CPI values come from?
The tool bundles no CPI data; enter officially published indices (such as DGBAS CPI) and label the periods.
Can the rate be negative?
Yes; a rate above −100% models deflation — the future nominal amount shrinks and purchasing power rises.
What is the equivalent annual rate?
The constant yearly rate that compounds to the total CPI change; it needs the years between the two indices.
