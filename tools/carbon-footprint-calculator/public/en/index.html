How the calculation works

Electricity = monthly kWh × 12 × 0.471; driving = distance × fuel use ÷ 100 × 2.31 (gasoline) or 2.68 (diesel); bus = distance × 0.102; metro / rail = distance × 0.035; flights = short-haul distance × 0.153 + long-haul distance × 0.148; diet = each weekly kg × 52 × its factor, summed. The total is the unrounded category sum rounded half-up to 1 decimal — not the sum of the rounded categories; tonnes are the unrounded total ÷ 1000, rounded; each share is the unrounded category ÷ the unrounded total × 100, rounded; with all-zero input every share is 0.0. Annual conversion is fixed at monthly × 12 and weekly × 52.

All calculation happens locally on this page. Nothing is uploaded, stored, or logged. The result is an estimate for this one year only; there is no long-term tracking.

Frequently asked questions

Is this a formal carbon inventory?
No, it is an estimate for personal reference only.
Why is the beef factor so high?
Diet factors are Poore & Nemecek (2018) global averages including land-use change; production methods vary widely, so treat the figures as comparative estimates.
How do I count a scooter?
Use the same vehicle fields with your scooter's distance and fuel use.
