How it is calculated and formatted

The daily target is body weight times the chosen factor; the unrounded product is rounded half-up to whole grams, and that is the only rounding step. The range is not the target plus or minus a fixed amount: it recomputes the same product at the factor minus 0.2 and plus 0.2 g/kg. With a custom factor of 0.1 the low factor clamps to 0, so the range starts at 0 g.

The per-meal split divides the daily target evenly; any remainder is added one gram at a time to the earliest meals, so the meals always sum to the daily target. The report protein-intake-report.json is the result in a fixed JSON format that you can download and keep. Numbers never use thousands separators.
