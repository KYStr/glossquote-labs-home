Food plan tables and fixed rules

Costs come from the USDA Food Plans, October 2026 (US average, four-person household baseline), assuming all meals and snacks are prepared at home; Alaska and Hawaii have separate prices and are out of scope.

Household size factors: 1 person ×1.20; 2 persons ×1.10; 3 persons ×1.05; 4 persons ×1.00; 5–6 persons ×0.95; 7 or more persons ×0.90.

Weekly total = unadjusted total × size factor, rounded half-up to the nearest $0.10 (an exact halfway value rounds up; e.g. $143.55 becomes $143.60). Monthly total = weekly total × 4.333, rounded half-up to the nearest $0.10; per-person weekly, per-person monthly, and daily average are rounded half-up to the cent.

Reference family of four (male and female 20–50, children 6–8 and 9–11) on Thrifty totals $236.50 per week, exactly matching USDA's published figure.

Frequently asked questions

Why does the monthly total differ slightly from USDA's published figure?
This tool multiplies the published rounded weekly total by 4.333; USDA's own monthly figures use unrounded weekly costs, so the two can differ by about $0.20.
Does it include eating out?
No. The food plans assume all meals and snacks are prepared at home.
Why does a one-person household cost more per person?
USDA adds 20% for one person and 10% for two, because small households get less bulk-buying benefit; households of seven or more get 10% off.
Are Moderate-Cost or Liberal plans included?
This tool offers only the Thrifty and Low-Cost plans.
