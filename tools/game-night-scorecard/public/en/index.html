Game Night Scorecard

Enter the players and each round's integer scores, pick high-score or low-score wins and how many rounds to drop, then press “Update round standings” to get kept totals, best kept rounds, dropped rounds and shared ranks.

Players

2–12 players. One short name per player (1–24 characters, first character a letter or digit); duplicates are not allowed.

Rules
Who wins

0–3 rounds, and fewer than the number of rounds. Each player drops their own worst rounds.

Round scores

1–30 rounds. Each round needs a name and one integer score per player (-999–999, negatives allowed).

The sample is fixed placeholder data (high-score wins, drop 1, A and B tie on kept total and are split by best round); press “Update round standings” to compute it.
