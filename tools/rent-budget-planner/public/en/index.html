Rent Budget Planner

Start from your monthly take-home income and a rent ratio you choose to find the most rent you can afford. Then, for one candidate home, work out the full move-in cash (first month, deposit and agency fee) and what is left each month after rent. Everything runs offline on this page; nothing is uploaded or stored.

Input format and limits
  • Amounts are whole New Taiwan dollars only: no separators, signs or decimals. Income up to 999,999,999; candidate rent up to 99,999,999.
  • The custom ratio is only used when the ratio is set to Custom: a whole number from 1 to 100. Otherwise the field is ignored.
  • Deposit and agency-fee months are planning parameters. The defaults (2 months deposit, half a month agency fee) are common Taiwan market rates, not signing advice.

Frequently asked questions

How does the 33⅓ preset work?

It takes exactly one third of income, rounded down to whole dollars so it never exceeds the ratio.

Is the half-month agency fee fixed?

No — you can set 0 or 1 month; half a month is a common Taiwan market rate and this is an estimate only.
