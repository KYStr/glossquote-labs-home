Frequently asked questions

Why lower the temperature for a fan oven?

Moving air heats more efficiently, so the convention is 20 °C lower (25 °F for Fahrenheit recipes); this tool applies the adjustment for the entered unit before converting.

What if the temperature is outside the chart?

Below Gas Mark ¼ (110 °C) or above Gas Mark 10 (260 °C), Celsius and Fahrenheit still convert by formula and the Gas Mark is shown as out of range.

Conversion happens on this device; nothing is sent or stored. The Gas Mark chart is the widely published standard chart; the fan adjustment is a baking convention — follow your recipe and oven manual for final settings.
