        How the pick is made

        • Every option gets an equally sized slice of the wheel and exactly the same probability of being picked. There are no weights.
        • The draw uses the browser's crypto.getRandomValues to produce 32-bit random numbers, with rejection sampling discarding the values that would cause bias, so every option's probability is exactly equal.
        • The result is drawn before the wheel animation starts; the animation only shows you the outcome and cannot change which slice was picked.
        • An option entered twice keeps two slices, so it has exactly twice the chance of an option entered once — this rule is stated openly and duplicates are never merged silently.
        • In elimination mode, the last entry left is called the “final remaining” entry; it was not drawn and is never listed as a pick.

        This tool is for everyday low-stakes choices only. It is not for gambling, admissions, employment, or any decision involving money or rights. Your options stay on this page: they are never stored, never uploaded, and never appear in the URL.
