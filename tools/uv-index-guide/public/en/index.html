Categories and calculation rules
  • Categories follow the WHO “Global Solar UV Index: A Practical Guide” thresholds and colours, with Chinese names from the Central Weather Administration: 0–2 Low (green, PMS 375), 3–5 Moderate (yellow, PMS 102), 6–7 High (orange, PMS 151), 8–10 Very high (red, PMS 032), 11+ Extreme (purple, PMS 265). The category is judged after rounding the UV index to the nearest integer (0.5 rounds up).
  • The UV index is 0 to 20 with at most 1 decimal place. Leading zeros, exponents, signs and spaces are not accepted. Each field holds at most 8 characters.
  • The minimum SPF depends on the category only: SPF 15 for Low, SPF 30 for Moderate and High, SPF 50 for Very high and Extreme; reapply every 2 hours from Moderate up. Avoid being outside during midday hours at 8 and above.
  • Time to redness is computed from the unprotected, unrounded index: minutes = MED ÷ (1.5 × UV index), rounded to whole minutes. There is no estimate when the index is 0; values above 180 minutes are shown as “180+ minutes”. MED values are approximate typical values: I 200, II 250, III 300, IV 450, V 600, VI 1000 J/m².
  • The CWA burn reference is the table entry “burn possible within N minutes”: High 30 minutes, Very high 20 minutes, Extreme 15 minutes; Low and Moderate have no entry. It is a reference value for an average unprotected person, not a safe time.
  • Data stays in the current page draft only; leaving or switching language discards it. Nothing is stored or uploaded.

FAQ

Do I need protection at UV index 3 and above?

Yes — WHO advises sun protection from UV index 3.

Is the time to redness a safe time?

No — it is an estimate of when redness appears without protection; individual variation is large and it is not a guarantee of how long you can stay in the sun.

Why does WHO not recommend reporting burn times?

The WHO guide states that reporting burn times is not recommended, because individual variation is large and the number is easily mistaken for a safe exposure time. This tool therefore only reports an estimate, with a disclaimer.
