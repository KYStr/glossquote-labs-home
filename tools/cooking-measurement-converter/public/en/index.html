Frequently asked questions

How many millilitres is a cup here?
A pinned 240 ml (the FDA household measure); the exact US customary cup is 236.588 ml and is not used.
Why isn't a cup of water 240 grams?
The density table follows one source, King Arthur's ingredient weight chart, which lists water at 227 g per cup; pure volume conversions never use the table.
What if my ingredient is not listed?
Weight-to-volume conversion only works for the 12 table ingredients; for volume-only conversions choose "None".
