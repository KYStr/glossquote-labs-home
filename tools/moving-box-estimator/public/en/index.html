How it is calculated

Base box counts are fixed per home type: studio 15, one-bedroom 25, two-bedroom 40, three-bedroom 60, four-bedroom 80 (total boxes in medium-box equivalents). The base is split small 30% / medium 50%, with small and medium rounded up and large taking the remainder, so the three sizes always add up to the base.

Additions are counted after the split and before the uplift: lots of books +8 small, many fragile items +4 small, large kitchen +6 medium, lots of clothes +5 medium. With “lived here 5+ years” on, the subtotal including additions is raised by 20%, rounded up, and the extra boxes all count as medium. Supplies follow one roll per 10 boxes, rounded up: tape and packing paper alike, with one extra packing-paper roll when there are many fragile items. These numbers are product design constants, not regulations or official statistics.

Frequently asked questions

What goes in each size?
Small boxes are for books and heavy items, medium for general household goods, and large for light, bulky items like clothes and bedding.
Why does living 5+ years add 20%?
Homes accumulate belongings over time, so this option raises the subtotal by 20%, rounded up, with the extra boxes counted as medium. Turn it off if you own less than typical.
