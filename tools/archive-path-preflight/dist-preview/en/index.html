Archive Member Path Preflight

Check a pasted archive manifest for unsafe relative names, case aliases, duplicate members and file-parent conflicts. Only the list you paste is checked: no archive is read, nothing is renamed, nothing is uploaded.

Input

One member per row: write F (file) or D (directory), then one Tab, then the relative path. At most 500 rows; each path is 1–240 characters with at most 16 segments.

Portable relative-path rules
  • Each row: F or D, one Tab, then the path. Paths have no trailing slash; the kind marks directories.
  • Paths are 1–240 characters with at most 16 segments; each segment is 1–64 characters and only accepts A–Z a–z 0–9 . _ -.
  • Absolute paths, drive letters, backslashes, . or .. segments, empty segments, and trailing dots are rejected.
  • Reserved device names are rejected: CON PRN AUX NUL COM1–COM9 LPT1–LPT9 (case-insensitive, compared before the extension).
  • Comparison folds ASCII case: paths that differ only in capitalisation conflict.
  • Declaring the same member twice, or a file occupying another member’s ancestor directory, is a conflict.
  • No archive is read or extracted, nothing is renamed, and no repair commands are produced.
