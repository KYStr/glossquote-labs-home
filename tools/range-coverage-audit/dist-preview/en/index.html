Format and limits
  • Number format: -?(0|[1-9][0-9]{0,9})(\.[0-9]{1,3})? — no surrounding whitespace, plus sign, or exponent; range -1,000,000,000 to 1,000,000,000.
  • Intervals text is at most 16,384 characters and 200 rows; each row is exactly “start Tab end”, with start smaller than end and inside the domain (never clipped).
  • Intervals are half-open [start, end): touching endpoints do not overlap, and the same interval twice has depth 2.
  • The report is range-coverage-audit-report.json, capped at 2,097,152 bytes; over the cap no download is offered.
  • Input is never uploaded, stored, or placed in the URL or logs; leaving the page discards it.

Frequently asked questions

Do touching endpoints overlap?
The right endpoint is excluded, so touching ranges do not overlap.
Is a three-way overlap counted three times?
Its length is counted once, with depth shown as three.
