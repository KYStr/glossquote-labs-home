How it is calculated

The division rule is fixed: a child who has turned six on or before September 1 of a school year enters elementary grade 1 that September. When the birth (month, day) falls on or before September 1, the entry year is the birth year plus 6; from September 2 onward it is the birth year plus 7. The age at entry is always 6 years plus a number of months counted from the observed sixth birthday to September 1 of the entry year.

A school year runs from September 1 to the next August 31 and is named by its starting year; the ROC year is the Gregorian year minus 1911. Kindergarten classes are divided by age reached on September 1: age 2 enters the toddler class, age 3 the junior class, age 4 the middle class, and age 5 the senior class, so the four entry years are 4, 3, 2, and 1 years before the elementary entry year. A February 29 birthday is observed on March 1 in non-leap years. The projection always lists all twelve years from elementary grade 1 to senior high grade 3, regardless of the as-of date.

Frequently asked questions

Why does one day change the cohort?
Children who have turned six on or before September 1 enter that September; a September 2 birthday waits until the next September.
Born on February 29?
In non-leap years the birthday is observed on March 1; entry still turns on being six by September 1 of the entry year.
