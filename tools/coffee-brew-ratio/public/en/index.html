How it is calculated and formatted

A ratio of 1:R means R grams of water for every 1 gram of coffee. From a dose, total water = dose × R; from total water, dose = total water ÷ R. Both results are rounded to the nearest 0.1 (halves round up). Bloom water is the solved dose times the bloom multiplier.

The water left after blooming is split into three equal pours; leftover 0.1 ml units go to the earlier pours. Each step shows its own pour amount and the cumulative target, and the final cumulative target always equals the total water exactly. Grams and millilitres always show one decimal place. This tool creates no export file; the results are plain selectable text on the page.

Frequently asked questions

What does 1:15 mean?
1 g of coffee to 15 g of water; this tool treats 1 ml of water as 1 g.
How is bloom water calculated?
Dose × the bloom multiplier (default 2×, adjustable from 0 to 5×).
How are the pours split?
The water left after blooming is split into three equal pours; leftover 0.1 ml units go to the earlier pours, so the final cumulative target equals the total exactly.
