Cash Float Tray

At close of trade, keep a target amount of cash in the tray from the denominations you actually counted, and remove the rest by piece count. Use one smallest unit for every amount (for example, all in dollars — do not mix in cents). The calculation happens only on this page; nothing is uploaded or stored.

Target and counted denominations

For example, enter 1000 to keep 1000. 0 means remove everything.
