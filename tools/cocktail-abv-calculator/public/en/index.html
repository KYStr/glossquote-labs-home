How it is calculated

Total ABV is the total pure-alcohol volume divided by the total volume; each ingredient's pure-alcohol volume is its volume × ABV ÷ 100. Pure-alcohol grams are pure-alcohol volume × 0.789 (ethanol density 0.789 g/ml at 20 °C); standard drinks are pure-alcohol grams ÷ 10 (in Taiwan, one standard drink is 10 g of pure alcohol). Volumes are summed additively; mixing contraction is not deducted. All values are rounded half-up to 1 decimal place, once, at output.

All calculation happens locally on this page. Nothing is uploaded, stored, or logged. The result covers this one mixed drink only and does not estimate blood alcohol concentration.
