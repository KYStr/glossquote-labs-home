Import timestamps

Up to 512 values, strictly increasing. A successful measurement replaces the current tap record with these timestamps.

How it works and its limits

  • The BPM comes from the median of the last at most 8 intervals in the current segment; the mean BPM uses the same intervals and is shown alongside.
  • A gap of more than 2000 ms between taps starts a new segment: that tap is the first tap of the new segment and earlier intervals no longer count.
  • Tap times stay in this page's memory only — nothing is uploaded or stored, and leaving the page discards them.
  • This tool only measures; it plays no click and records no audio. Copy the BPM into your metronome yourself.

Why the median?

One off tap does not drag the reading; the mean is shown alongside for comparison.

What happens after a long pause?

A gap over 2000 ms starts a new segment: that tap is the first tap of the new segment and earlier intervals no longer count.
