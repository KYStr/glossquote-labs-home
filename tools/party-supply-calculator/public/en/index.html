Rules and limits
  • Guests 1 to 500; party duration 0.5 to 12 hours in 0.5-hour steps; meal type snacks, light meal or full meal.
  • Food per person: snacks 200 g, light meal 350 g, full meal 500 g, times the duration factor (1.0 up to 2 hours, 1.2 for 2–4 hours, 1.5 over 4 hours).
  • Drinks use the 2-1 rule: 2 servings per person in the first hour, 1 per hour after, at 350 ml per serving.
  • Cups: 2 per person up to 3 hours, otherwise 3 per person. Plates: 1 per person for snacks or light meal, 2 for a full meal, plus 20% spare in the total. Ice: 0.5 kg per person, times the duration factor.
  • Totals are always rounded up to buyable units: food to 100 g, drinks to 500 ml, ice to 0.5 kg; drinks are also converted to 2-litre bottles.
  • Data stays only in the current page draft; leaving or switching languages discards it. Nothing is stored or uploaded.

FAQ

Are these quantities guaranteed?

No. They are planning estimates from common catering rules of thumb; adjust for your menu and guests.

Why 2 drinks in the first hour and 1 per hour after?

That is the caterers’ 2-1 rule: 2 servings per person in the first hour, 1 per hour after, at 350 ml per serving.

Where does 0.5 kg of ice per person come from?

It is the common rule of about 1 lb of ice per person, times 1.5 for parties over 4 hours.
