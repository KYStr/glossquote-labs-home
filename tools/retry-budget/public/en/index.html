How it is calculated, and the report format

The first attempt has no delay: it starts at 0 ms and ends at the per-attempt timeout. For attempt i (i ≥ 2), the delay is the initial delay multiplied by the factor to the power of i−2, capped at the delay cap; the delay is added after the previous attempt ends. The first attempt whose end exceeds the total budget is not kept, and its waiting time is not deducted; an attempt ending exactly at the budget fits. Unspent time is the budget minus the end of the last kept attempt, or 0 when nothing was kept.

Every field accepts ASCII decimal integer strings only, with no spaces, signs, decimals, or exponents, and at most 8 digits per field. The downloaded report is always named retry-budget-report.json and contains the result as compact JSON, with keys sorted by UTF-16 code unit order and one trailing newline.
