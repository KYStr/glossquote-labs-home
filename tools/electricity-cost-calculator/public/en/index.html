Formulas and rounding

Energy per day (kWh) = watts ÷ 1,000 × hours per day × quantity; energy per period = energy per day × days; cost = energy × rate. In tiered mode the period energy is priced band by band, and the per-day figure is the period cost divided by the days — an average.

Costs are computed from the unrounded energy and rounded only once, for display. So a displayed per-day cost multiplied by the days may differ from the period cost by a cent or two; the period figure is the exact one. Thousands are grouped with commas and the decimal separator is a point. The period cost is the price of the usage you entered at the rates you entered — not a prediction of your whole bill. Taxes and fixed monthly fees are not included; you can fold them into your rate yourself.
