Barcode Generator

Turn a number into a printable, scannable 1D barcode. Code 128 suits internal codes (SKUs, assets, shelf labels); EAN-13 and UPC-A are the retail number formats, with the check digit computed or validated in the open. The module count, quiet zones and the physical print width at your chosen module width are stated beside the preview, and the PNG/SVG downloads contain exactly the bars on screen. Everything runs locally - nothing is uploaded or stored.

Please note: retail EAN/UPC numbers are allocated by GS1. This tool only performs the check-digit and bar arithmetic. A number you invent yourself is fine for internal use; do not present it as a registered product barcode.
