FAQ

Why does real charging take longer?

This tool assumes constant power for the whole session; real charging usually tapers at higher charge levels, so actual time is often longer.

What is wall energy?

The energy drawn from the grid. Charging is not lossless, so wall energy = battery energy / efficiency.
