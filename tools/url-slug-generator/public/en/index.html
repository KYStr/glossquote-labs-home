Conversion rules and limits
  • The title is required, at most 1000 characters. Max length is optional, default 50, range 1 to 100; leaving it blank applies the default 50. Max length accepts whole numbers only — no decimals, signs or spaces.
  • Conversion runs in order: NFKD normalization (fullwidth letters and digits fold to ASCII, ligatures expand, accented letters split apart) → combining marks U+0300–U+036F are removed → the closed special-letter table applies (æ→ae, œ→oe, ø→o, đ→d, ð→d, þ→th, ł→l, ß→ss, in both cases) → lowercase → each run of characters outside a–z and 0–9 becomes one hyphen → leading and trailing hyphens are removed.
  • Chinese characters are not converted to pinyin; they are treated as separators and dropped. A title with only Chinese characters produces no slug and returns an error — it is never replaced with “untitled”.
  • Underscores, spaces, punctuation and emoji are all separators and become hyphens; digits are kept. When the slug is longer than the cap it is cut to the first maxLength characters and any trailing hyphen left by the cut is removed; a slug exactly at the cap is not truncated.
  • Data stays in the current page draft only; leaving or switching language discards it. Nothing is stored or uploaded.

FAQ

Are Chinese titles converted to pinyin?

No, Chinese characters are treated as separators and dropped; a title with only Chinese characters returns an error, so add Latin letters or digits.

Are underscores kept?

No, underscores are separators and become hyphens.

Can the slug exceed the length cap?

No, it is truncated and any trailing hyphen left by truncation is removed.

Does this check whether the slug is already in use?

No, it only converts the text; check duplicates in your own site admin.
