    The screen is fixed at 16:9: width = diagonal × 2.54 × 16/√337, height = diagonal × 2.54 × 9/√337 (1 inch = 2.54 cm). Each distance is diagonal × multiplier × 2.54, computed in exact decimal arithmetic before any rounding. In inverse mode the distance is first converted to inches, then divided by the ideal 1.5–2.0, minimum and maximum multipliers to give the range and its bounds. The downloaded report is always named tv-viewing-distance-report.json and holds the result as compact JSON with keys sorted by UTF-16 code units and one trailing newline.

    FAQ

    Are these distances exact viewing-angle conversions?
    No. They are fixed rules of thumb based on THX (about 36–40°) and SMPTE (about 30°) viewing-angle guidance: minimum 1.2×, ideal 1.5–2.0× and maximum 2.5× the diagonal.
    Why can you sit closer with 4K?
    At a higher resolution pixels are harder to see at the same distance, so this tool sets the 4K minimum-distance multiplier to 1.0. The ideal and maximum distances do not change with resolution.
