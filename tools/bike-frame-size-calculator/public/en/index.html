Calculation rules and limits
  • Height 120 to 220 cm, inseam 50 to 110 cm, inseam must not exceed height. Numbers are decimals with at most 1 decimal place; no thousands commas, exponents, signs, or spaces; at most 6 characters per field.
  • Formulas: road frame = inseam × 0.67 (Guimard, 1980s); hybrid = inseam × 0.64 (city / trekking geometry); mountain = road frame − 11 cm; starting saddle height = inseam × 0.883 (LeMond method, measured from the bottom-bracket centre along the seat tube to the top of the saddle). 1 inch = 2.54 cm exactly.
  • The output is always a range, never a single number: the recommended range is the centre ± 1.0 cm. The inch figures are arithmetic conversions of the centimetre figures, not shop labels.
  • Letter size comes from height (half-open intervals): under 150 cm XXS, 150–160 XS, 160–168 S, 168–178 M, 178–185 L, 185–193 XL, 193 cm or taller XXL. The letter is a height reference, not a frame-centimetre reference.
  • An inseam-to-height ratio inside 40% to 50% inclusive gives no warning; outside it only warns to re-measure and does not block the result. Values round half-up (0.5 up) to 1 decimal place, the ratio to 3 decimal places, only at output.
  • How to measure (book method): barefoot, back against a wall, feet about 15–20 cm apart, push a hardcover book spine-up into your crotch with saddle-like pressure, and measure from the floor to the top of the book spine. Measure twice and average. A trouser inseam runs about 1–2 inches shorter than a cycling inseam; do not enter it directly.

FAQ

Can I order this size directly?

No — this is a starting point. Brands differ in geometry; confirm against the brand size chart and a test ride.

Is the inch figure the shop size?

No — it is the arithmetic conversion of the centimetre figure. Road and hybrid bikes are not sold by inches in shops.

Can I enter my trouser inseam?

No — a trouser inseam runs about 1–2 inches shorter than a cycling inseam. Re-measure with the book method.

How do I measure my inseam?

Barefoot, back against a wall, feet about 15–20 cm apart, push a hardcover book spine-up into your crotch with saddle-like pressure, and measure from the floor to the top of the book spine. Measure twice and average.
