Statutory days and legal basis

Annual leave table, Labor Standards Act Article 38

TenureLeave days
Under 6 months0 days
6 months to under 1 year3 days
1 year7 days
2 years10 days
3 years to under 5 years14 days
5 years to under 10 years15 days
10 years or more1 extra day per year, up to 30 days

Legal basis: Article 38 of the Labor Standards Act (version effective January 1, 2017). This tool estimates days from tenure and dates only; it does not cover company calendar-year cycles, deferrals or expiry payouts, half-day or hourly units, separation settlements, or company extras. Dates are Asia/Taipei calendar days; years are limited to 1900–2100.

Frequently asked questions

Started on Feb 29?
In non-leap years the anniversary falls on Feb 28.
Which law sets the days?
The tenure table in Article 38 of the Labor Standards Act (version effective Jan 1, 2017).
Is this an official leave system?
No — it estimates statutory days from dates only; deferrals, payouts and company extras are not included.
