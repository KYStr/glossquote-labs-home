How it is calculated, and the fixed rules

Required wattage = converted area × W/m² × (ceiling height / 2.4), rounded up to the next multiple of 10 W; above 3000 W, use multiple 2000 W units. The W/m² table (good 60 / average 80 / poor 130) comes from the Adax by Solaire buying guide's published rule of thumb, not an engineering heat-loss standard. 1 坪 = 400/121 m² (about 3.305785 m²).

All calculation happens locally on this page: nothing is uploaded, stored, or logged. The result covers this one estimate only, with no long-term tracking.

Frequently asked questions

How is 坪 converted?
1 坪 = 400/121 m² (about 3.305785 m²).
Is this a professional heat-loss calculation?
No, it is a rule-of-thumb estimate by insulation level, for sizing guidance only; consult a professional for large rooms, high ceilings or unusual layouts.
Why is the recommended size larger than the required wattage?
Heaters are sold in fixed wattage steps, so the recommendation is the smallest step at or above the required wattage; above 3000 W, use multiple 2000 W units.
