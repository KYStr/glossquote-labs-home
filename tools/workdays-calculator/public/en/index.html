Rules and limits

A business day is a Monday to Friday that is not in your custom holiday list; weekends are always Saturday and Sunday. Counting excludes the start date and includes the end date: swapping the two dates only negates the business-day count, while the breakdown is always computed on the forward range. No national holidays are built in.

Input limits: dates from 1900-01-01 to 2100-12-31; business days from -3660 to 3660; at most 400 holiday lines and 4096 characters of holiday text. The two modes are exact inverses: when the end date is a business day, adding the counted business days to the start date lands back on the end date.

Frequently asked questions

How are the endpoints counted?
The start date is excluded and the end date is included: Monday to Friday counts 4 business days (Tue–Fri), and adding 4 business days to that Monday lands back on the Friday.
Is a holiday on a weekend subtracted twice?
No. Weekend days are never business days; such holidays are reported separately and never double-counted.
Are national holidays built in?
No. Holidays are the dates you enter yourself, one per line; no official calendar is included.
