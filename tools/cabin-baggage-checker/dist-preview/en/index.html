Cabin Baggage Checker

Enter your main bag's three dimensions and weight (personal item optional) to check it against six airlines' economy-class (international) carry-on rules at once: the three sides are sorted and compared side by side, weight is judged by each airline's own rule, and every airline gets a pass/fail with margins. The comparison uses a rules table verified on 2026-11-09, not a live lookup.

Baggage and airlines

Main bag size (cm): length, width, height

The three sides can be entered in any order; the tool sorts them before comparing. Dimensions include wheels, handles and side pockets; each side must be over 0 and at most 200 cm, with at most one decimal place.

Over 0 and at most 100 kg, with at most one decimal place.

Personal item (optional): length, width, height, weight

Leave all four blank if you have no personal item; if any one is filled in, all four are required. Dimension rules match the main bag; weight is at most 100 kg.

How the check works and about the rules table

Your three sides are sorted from largest to smallest and compared side by side with each airline's limits; any side over its limit fails the size check. This tool does not apply the airlines' alternative "sum of three sides ≤ 115 cm" rule. Weight comes in two kinds: EVA Air and China Airlines apply 7 kg to the main bag alone (main-bag rule); STARLUX applies 7 kg, and Tigerair Taiwan, ANA and JAL apply 10 kg, to the main bag plus personal item combined (total rule). Personal-item limits: EVA Air and STARLUX 40×30×10 cm, China Airlines 40×30×15 cm, ANA 40×30×20 cm; Tigerair Taiwan and JAL publish no dimensions, shown as "not specified" and never affecting the verdict. ANA and JAL figures are for international aircraft with 100+ seats. The report cabin-baggage-report.json is the result in a fixed JSON format, available for download. All numbers carry exactly one decimal place, without thousands separators.

Frequently asked questions

Should I include wheels and handles?
Yes. All six airlines count wheels, handles and side pockets; measure the outside of the packed bag.
Why is STARLUX stricter on weight?
STARLUX caps the main bag and personal item together at 7 kg in Economy, while EVA Air and China Airlines apply 7 kg to the main bag alone.
Does the order of the three sides matter?
No. The tool sorts your three sides from largest to smallest and compares them side by side with each airline's limits.
