How conversion works and the fixed standard table

Every conversion anchors on the inside circumference in mm: an input in any system is first converted to a circumference, and all outputs are derived from that circumference alone, never from the original input. The EU size is the ISO 8653 whole-millimetre label of the circumference; the Japan size is derived from the JIS S 4700 diameter definition (size 1 has a 13.0 mm inside diameter, each size adds 1/3 mm, so Japan size = round(3 × diameter − 38)); US and UK sizes come only from the fixed 21-row table below (US 3.0–13.0 in half steps) and are never interpolated.

The nearest standard size is the table row closest to your circumference; an exact midpoint takes the larger row. A difference of up to 0.30 mm counts as an exact match; beyond that the result says “between sizes” and shows the adjacent row too. Quarter US sizes (such as 6.25), circumferences outside 40.0–70.0 mm, and other systems such as Hong Kong or China sizes are not supported. The downloaded report is always named ring-size-report.json.

Frequently asked questions

How is the Japan size derived?
From the JIS S 4700 diameter definition: size 1 has a 13.0 mm inside diameter and each size adds 1/3 mm. This tool converts the circumference to a diameter and rounds; brands may differ by one size.
What if the result says "between sizes"?
When the circumference is more than 0.3 mm from the nearest standard size, the adjacent size is shown too. Most wearers size up, and a physical measurement decides.
