Laundry Machine Handoff

Enter the day's working window, both machines' busy intervals and each load's wash and dry minutes, then press “Plan washer-to-dryer slots” to get every load's wash, wait and dry times.

Working window

Start and end of the same day. Start 00:00–23:59, end 00:00–24:00.

Washer busy intervals

Up to 16 intervals per machine. Half-open [start, end); touching intervals do not overlap. Intervals may extend outside the window; blank rows are ignored.

Dryer busy intervals

Up to 16 intervals per machine. Half-open [start, end); touching intervals do not overlap. Intervals may extend outside the window; blank rows are ignored.

Laundry loads

1–8 loads, scheduled in input order. One short name per load (1–24 characters, first character a letter or digit); names must be unique.

The sample is fixed placeholder data (washer busy until 08:20, dryer busy until 09:10, first load starts washing at 08:30); press “Plan washer-to-dryer slots” to compute it.
