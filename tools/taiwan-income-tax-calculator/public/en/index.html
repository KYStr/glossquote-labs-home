FAQ

Below what salary does a single filer owe nothing?

Below NT$446,000 — the salary special deduction (218,000), one exemption (97,000) and the single standard deduction (131,000) total 446,000.

Is a spouse's salary included?

No. This version counts only the filer's salary; a spouse affects the standard deduction and exemptions only.
