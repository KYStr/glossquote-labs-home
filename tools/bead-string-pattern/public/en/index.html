Bead String Pattern

Enter the colors you have and how many beads of each, pick a motif, the number of repeats and straight or mirrored alternation; choose “Lay out the bead order” to get the bead-by-bead order, the usage per color and the first position that runs short.

Color stock

Up to 12 colors. Each color needs a short name and a bead count; count 0–1000. Short names are 1–24 characters, starting with a letter or digit.

Motif

Up to 16 beads. Pick a color from the stock for each position; the same color may repeat.

Repeats and end beads

1–32 groups.

Layout

The sample is fixed placeholder data (R, G, B mirrored over two groups, with a K bead at each end); after loading, choose “Lay out the bead order” to calculate.
