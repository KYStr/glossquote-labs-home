How it works and its limits

The circumference combination is formed in centimetres first (men: waist − neck; women: waist + hip − neck), then divided by 2.54 into inches for the US Navy circumference method (Hodgdon & Beckett 1984) log10 formulas with their original constants; height is converted the same way. Body fat percentage, fat mass and lean mass are shown to one decimal place, and the category follows the ACE body fat percentage table applied to the rounded percentage. The download is always named body-fat-report.json.

Frequently asked questions

How accurate is this method?
The circumference method is an estimate and differs from professional scans such as DEXA; it is best used to track trends.
Why do men not enter hip?
The US Navy formula uses waist and neck for men; hip is only part of the women's formula.
