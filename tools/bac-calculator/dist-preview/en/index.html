The administrative threshold follows Article 35 of the Road Traffic Management and Penalty Act and Article 114 of the Road Traffic Safety Rules; the criminal threshold follows Article 185-3 of the Criminal Code. The breath figure is converted at a blood-to-breath ratio of 2000:1, a reference value derived from the statutes' paired thresholds, not an instrument reading.

How it is calculated, and the input format

The order is fixed: total pure alcohol in grams is the sum over drinks of volume × ABV × 0.789 g/ml; the pre-elimination concentration is the pure alcohol divided by (distribution factor × body weight), with the factor 0.68 for men and 0.55 for women; the current concentration is the pre-elimination concentration minus linear elimination at 0.15 g/L per hour, floored at zero. Percent is the concentration divided by 10, and the breath figure is the concentration divided by 2. All computation uses exact rational arithmetic, never floating point; each displayed value rounds half-up exactly once, while the legal band and the remaining minutes are always decided and computed from the unrounded exact value.

Remaining minutes are the smallest whole number of minutes needed for the concentration to fall to the target: 400 minutes per g/L, rounded up. Inputs accept ASCII digits and at most one decimal point: commas, spaces, signs, exponents and full-width digits are format errors, and the tool never guesses. The downloaded report is always named bac-report.json; its content is the result as compact JSON with object keys sorted recursively by UTF-16 code units and one trailing newline.

Frequently asked questions

Is below 0.15 mg/L OK to drive?
No. The threshold is only where penalties start; alcohol impairs reaction and judgment from the first drink, and this tool is only an estimate — error could put you over the threshold in reality.
Can water, coffee or exercise speed up elimination?
No. Alcohol is eliminated at a roughly fixed 0.15 g/L per hour; water, coffee, cold showers and exercise do not speed it up. Only time works.
How is the breath figure converted?
Taiwan's statutes define thresholds in pairs — breath 0.15 mg/L with blood 0.03%, and breath 0.25 mg/L with blood 0.05% — both give a blood-to-breath ratio of 2000:1. This tool converts with the same ratio; the result is a reference value, not an instrument reading.

Everything is calculated locally in this page; your input is never uploaded, stored, or logged. Drinking data is sensitive personal data, and this tool never sends it out of the page.
