Taiwan Water Bill Calculator

Choose the meter-reading cycle and meter diameter, enter one period's water usage, and estimate the Taiwan Water Corporation basic charge, four-tier usage charge and water conservation levy. You can also add the sewer and waste rates from your own bill, per cubic metre.

This calculator covers Taiwan Water Corporation customers only; Taipei Water Department rates differ.

How it is calculated, and the rate table

The usage charge follows the official formula: unit price × degrees − progressive difference; the tier subtotals sum to the same value. The basic charge is looked up by reading cycle and meter diameter. The water conservation and feedback levy is 5% of the tax-excluded usage charge (the usage charge only, never the basic fee), rounded to the nearest yuan. The sewer and waste fees are the entered rate × degrees, rounded to the nearest yuan; if no rate is entered they are excluded from the total and shown as “Not included”. The total payable is the water subtotal rounded to the nearest yuan, plus the conservation levy, the sewer fee and the waste fee. Business tax, (basic charge + usage charge) ÷ 1.05 × 5%, is an information row only and is never added.

Frequently asked questions

Is this the Taipei tariff too?
No — Taipei Water Department uses its own rate table.
What is a “degree”?
One degree is 1 m³, i.e. 1,000 litres.
Why can my bill differ?
Sewer and waste rates vary by municipality and sewer connection and are excluded unless entered; estimated readings, special user classes and mid-period changes are not modelled.
How is the conservation levy computed?
5% of the tax-excluded usage charge (the usage charge only, never the basic fee), rounded to the nearest yuan.
