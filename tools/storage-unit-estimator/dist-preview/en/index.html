Storage Unit Estimator

Before moving or renovating, work out how much space your things need: add furniture, appliances and boxes row by row to get the total volume and the required volume including an aisle allowance, then a recommended storage unit size. This tool estimates volume; it is not a loading plan, and results are for choosing a unit size only.

Item list

This tool estimates volume; it is not a loading plan. Results are for choosing a unit size only — whether everything actually fits also depends on item shapes, door width, and how things are stacked.

How volume and the recommendation are calculated

Each item uses its pinned typical outer dimensions (length × width × height) converted to cubic metres, multiplied by quantity and summed; custom items use the dimensions you enter. The required volume is the total × 1.25: items are not perfect cubes, stacking leaves gaps, and you need an aisle inside the unit; the 25% is a fixed empirical allowance, not a measured packing efficiency. The recommended unit is the smallest of six storage sizes (5×5 to 10×30 ft, 8 ft high) whose capacity is at least the required volume; when even the largest single unit cannot hold everything, several largest units are suggested.

All calculation happens locally on this page. Nothing is uploaded, stored, or logged.

Frequently asked questions

How is volume calculated?
Each item uses its pinned typical outer dimensions (length × width × height) converted to cubic metres, multiplied by quantity and summed; custom items use the dimensions you enter.
Why is the required volume 25% more than the total?
Items are not perfect cubes, stacking leaves gaps, and you need an aisle inside the unit; the tool applies a fixed 25% allowance, not a measured packing efficiency.
Will the recommended unit definitely fit everything?
Not necessarily. The recommendation compares volumes only; shape, door width and stacking also matter. Measure the door and check with the facility for oversized or irregular items.
