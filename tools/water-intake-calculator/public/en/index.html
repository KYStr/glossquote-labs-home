The model

Baseline: 30–35 ml per kilogram of body weight. Activity additions (ml, added to both ends): Sedentary +0, Light +350, Moderate +700, High +1,050. Climate multipliers: Temperate ×1.0, Warm ×1.1, Hot ×1.25. Formula: low = (30 × weight in kg + activity addition) × climate multiplier; high = (35 × weight in kg + activity addition) × climate multiplier. Cups use 250 ml per cup and are computed from the exact, unrounded values.
