Text-to-Speech Reader

Paste text, pick one of the voices your browser offers, and hear it read aloud: the current sentence is highlighted as it is spoken, with play, pause, resume and stop, and playback from any sentence you click. Your text lives only in this tab's memory — it is never stored or uploaded. The one exception is a network voice: reading with one sends the text to that voice's provider, and the page says so as soon as you pick one.

This tool cannot record the reading to an audio file: the platform API cannot capture its own speech output, so there is deliberately no download button. Use recording or TTS software with export when you need a file.

This browser does not support speech synthesis

Your browser does not expose speechSynthesis, so nothing can be read aloud. Try a current version of Chrome, Edge, Firefox or Safari.

    Good to know

    • Changing the voice, rate or pitch during playback takes effect from the next chunk of text; Stop takes effect immediately.
    • Pause behaviour varies by platform: some local voices may ignore pause. Progress is computed per chunk, so the highlight and progress stay correct.
    • Whether reading continues when the tab is in the background is decided by the browser and OS; this tool cannot guarantee it.
    • Sentence splitting is a rule-based heuristic for highlight and chunking: an occasional over- or under-split changes only the highlight granularity, never the words read or their order.
    • The voice list is provided by your browser and operating system; this tool cannot add voices. If the list is empty, check that your system has voices installed.
    • Editing the text during playback stops reading at once, so a stale queue never keeps reading deleted text.
