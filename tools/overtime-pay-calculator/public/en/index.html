Overtime pay breakdown

How it is calculated, and the legal basis

This estimate assumes a monthly salary, an 8-hour normal workday, and a 240-hour monthly wage basis. The hourly rate is salary ÷ 240. One day's wage is salary ÷ 30. Each date is calculated separately. Weekday overtime pays 4/3× for the first 2 hours and 5/3× for the next 2. Actual rest-day work pays 4/3× for the first 2 hours, 5/3× for the next 6, and 8/3× for the next 4. Each eligible unshifted national holiday worked earns one extra day's wage. Hours beyond 8 use the weekday overtime bands.

The estimate uses exact fractions and rounds each daily amount half-up to whole NT dollars before summing the month. Counted overtime includes weekday overtime, actual rest-day hours, and holiday hours beyond 8. A notice appears above 46 counted hours. Weekly rest days, shifted holidays, compensatory time, and flexible schedules require separate assessment.

The download is named overtime-pay-report.json and contains the daily breakdown and monthly totals.

Frequently asked questions

How are rest-day hours counted?
Since 1 March 2018 the deemed-hours rule was abolished; rest-day pay and hours use actual hours worked.
How is the hourly rate derived?
This estimate divides monthly salary by 240, using an 8-hour normal workday.
What if holiday work is under 8 hours?
Each eligible unshifted national holiday worked earns one extra day's wage, even when you work fewer than 8 hours that day.
Why enter each date separately?
Rate bands start over each day. Three hours spread over three days can produce a different estimate from three hours on one day.

This estimate is for reference only; actual pay follows the employment agreement and the competent authority's determination. All calculation happens locally on this page. Nothing is uploaded, stored, or logged.
