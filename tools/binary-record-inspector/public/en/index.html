Binary Record Inspector

Paste a test fixture hex dump and a field table to compare both byte orders and signed readings of the same bytes. Everything runs locally; nothing is uploaded or stored.

Separate tokens with a space, tab, or newline; each token is an even number of hex digits. No 0x prefix or addresses; at most 4,096 bytes.

One row per field: “label ⇥ offset ⇥ type”, tab-separated; at most 64 rows. Types: u8, i8, u16le, u16be, i16le, i16be, u32le, u32be, i32le, i32be, u64le, u64be, i64le, i64be.

Limits

  • Hex bytes: at most 4,096 bytes; field layout: at most 64 rows.
  • Labels start with a letter, are at most 24 characters, and must be unique.
  • Offsets start at 0; a field must not run past the end of the bytes.
  • Two fields may share bytes; reading never edits the source.
  • The downloaded report always uses the fixed name binary-record-inspector-report.json.
Where does an offset start?
The first byte is at offset zero.
Can two fields overlap?
Each field reads its requested bytes without editing them.
