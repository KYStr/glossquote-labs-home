## A SPEC internal conflict, resolved toward its behavioural statements

SPEC section 4 names the fold **Unicode simple case folding** and
states behaviourally that İ (U+0130) "has no simple fold mapping
and is unchanged by the fold", that Σ and ς both fold to σ, and
that `I` folds to `i`. Its parenthetical "(status C and F
entries)" contradicts the İ statement: İ carries only an F (full
folding) entry (to `i` + U+0307), so under C+F the İ statement is
false, while under the defined simple fold (status C and S, S
overriding C) every behavioural statement in the SPEC holds,
including the sigma discriminator. The implementation ships C+S
and the conflict is recorded in `PROVENANCE.md` for the
bulk-review errata; switching the generator to F entries is a
one-line change if the SPEC is corrected the other way.

Two further interpretation points, both pinned by the SPEC's own
fixtures and recorded here rather than silently chosen:

- **Stopwords default off.** Fixture 1 counts `the` 3 times at
  30.0% of a 10-token total, which is impossible with the bundled
  English list applied; the SPEC never states the toggle default,
  so the fixture pins it: the bundled lists are prefilled in the
  editor but the toggle starts off.
- **Minimum count is a row threshold; the other filters are
  token-level.** Section 4 says totals are "totals after filters"
  and the Words table is what minimum count thresholds (it sits in
  the same display family as "displaying the first 100 rows unless
  Show all is on"); stopwords, minimum length and numbers-off
  exclude tokens before counting (so their totals shrink), while
  minimum count filters finished rows without changing the counted
  total. A test pins this reading.

Rows carry both the folded `key` and the display `item`: the
SPEC's fixture lists name folded keys (fixtures 4, 6, 7 say so
explicitly), while the display form follows the section 4
majority/first-occurrence rule (fixtures 1, 3, 5). The UI and CSV
show the display form; tests assert both.
