How it is calculated

Add overhead to the target annual income, then divide by billable hours (working days × utilization × hours per day) for the hourly rate, and by billable days for the day rate. The monthly target is the target annual income divided by 12. Amounts round half-up to whole NT$, days and hours to one decimal place; rates are computed from the unrounded exact values, never from already-rounded intermediates.

All calculation happens locally on this page. Nothing is uploaded, stored, or recorded. Currency is fixed to New Taiwan dollars (NT$).
