About this table

The tables here are transcribed from the ISO/TS 19407-based conversion tables in the Wikipedia article "Shoe size" (22 adult rows, 16 kids rows) and ship as a data file with its source, row counts and SHA-256 recorded; every answer on this page is read from a single row of that table, never computed from a formula.

Shoe sizing is not fully standardised: brands publish their own charts and their lasts differ. Treat a converted label as a starting point for trying shoes on, never as a promise of fit. This tool converts labels; it does not recommend a size to buy, and it carries no individual brand's chart.
