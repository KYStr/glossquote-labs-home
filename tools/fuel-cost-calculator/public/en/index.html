Fuel Cost Calculator

Enter the distance, your car's consumption and the fuel price to estimate how much fuel the trip takes and what it costs in total. Consumption can be given in L/100km, km/L, US MPG or UK MPG, and the price per litre, per US gallon or per Imperial gallon; you can also split the cost among the car's occupants. Everything is computed in your browser — nothing is uploaded or stored.

How it works

  • Results are estimates from the consumption figure you enter; real consumption varies with traffic, speed and driving style.
  • The currency selector changes the displayed symbol only and performs no exchange conversion; enter a price in the currency you select.
  • US and Imperial gallons differ: 1 US gallon = 3.785411784 litres and 1 Imperial gallon = 4.54609 litres. Always pick the right country for MPG — the two are never interchangeable.
  • Switching a unit never rescales the number you typed: the number stays as typed and is reinterpreted in the new unit. Check the converted-consumption line to sanity-check the result.
  • Amounts are rounded to cents only when displayed, never during the calculation, so the same inputs give the same cents everywhere.
