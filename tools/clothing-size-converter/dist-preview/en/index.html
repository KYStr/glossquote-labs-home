The full reference charts (fixed)

Both charts are fixed references, looked up cell by cell — never derived by formula or interpolation: 8 women's rows keyed by bust and 7 men's rows keyed by chest. Ranges are half-open: a measurement equal to a row's lower edge belongs to that row; a measurement equal to the chart's top edge is outside the chart. A “—” cell means that system has no label on that row and never takes part in a lookup. Men's UK numbers equal the US numbers (both are chest-inches labels), and the men's JP numeric column is always blank. The downloaded report is always named clothing-size-report.json.

Frequently asked questions

Why does the same size fit differently across brands?
Size charts vary by brand. This tool uses one fixed reference chart, so check the brand's official size chart and try the garment on whenever possible.
What happens when my measurement is exactly on a boundary?
Ranges are half-open: a measurement equal to a row's lower edge belongs to that row; a measurement equal to the chart's top edge is outside the chart.
Can I look up shoe or bottom sizes here?
No. This tool covers tops only; shoe sizes have their own converter, and bottoms are out of scope.
