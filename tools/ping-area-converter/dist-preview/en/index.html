How the conversion works and the fixed constants

Every conversion is exact rational arithmetic: 1 ping = 400/121 m² (1 尺 = 10/33 m, and a ping is a 6-尺 square); 1 ft² = 0.09290304 m² (the square of the exact 1 ft = 0.3048 m definition); 1 tatami = 1.62 m² is the Japanese real-estate advertising convention. Each output is rounded exactly once, at the end, round-half-up (an exact .5 rounds up): ping, m² and ft² to 2 decimal places, tatami to 1.

Physical tatami sizes vary by region — Kyoma ≈ 1.824 m², Chukyoma ≈ 1.656 m², Edoma ≈ 1.549 m², Danchima ≈ 1.445 m² — so this tool always converts tatami approximately at 1.62 m². Results are for everyday estimates only, not for registration or contracts.

Frequently asked questions

Why is 1 ping about 2 tatami when 1 tatami is 1.62 m²?
Physical tatami sizes vary by region (Kyoma ≈ 1.824 m², Edoma ≈ 1.549 m²); this tool uses the Japanese real-estate advertising convention 1 tatami = 1.62 m² as an approximate conversion, and 1 ping = 400/121 m² ÷ 1.62 ≈ 2.04 tatami.
Can I use the result in a contract or registration?
No. Registered areas are recorded in square metres and ping is a customary unit; this tool is for everyday estimates only — use the square metres on the official title for formal documents.
