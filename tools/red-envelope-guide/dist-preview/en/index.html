Suggested range and recommended amount

    Amount rules and the market tables

    A valid amount is at least NT$200, a multiple of 100, and its decimal string contains no digit 4; below NT$10,000 the hundreds digit must be 0, 2, 6 or 8, and at NT$10,000 or above the thousands figure must contain no 4 and end in 0, 2, 6 or 8. Both range ends and the recommended amount (the range midpoint) are snapped to the nearest valid amount, ties upward.

    Wedding amounts come from a relationship × venue-tier table (colleague NT$1,200–3,200, close friend NT$2,600–4,800, relative NT$2,600–7,800, elder/supervisor NT$2,000–4,800, rising with the tier). When attending with companions, each extra person adds a fixed increment to both ends (restaurant 1,000, hotel 1,200, five-star/premium 1,600). Not attending a wedding scales the range to 60% before snapping. Full-month and housewarming amounts look up by relationship, and a housewarming may instead be a gift of equivalent value; Lunar New Year amounts look up by the giver's stage (for elders) or the child's age band (for juniors).

    The tables are a Taiwan market reference compiled by the author (2026-11, v1); customs vary by region and family. The downloaded report is always named red-envelope-report.json and holds the result as compact JSON with object keys sorted by UTF-16 code units, ending with one newline.

    Frequently asked questions

    Why avoid the digit 4?
    The digit 4 is considered unlucky, and even hundreds — especially 2, 6 and 8 — are preferred.
    Why 60% when not attending?
    A guest who does not attend takes no seat or meal, so the customary amount is about 60%, then snapped to a valid amount.
